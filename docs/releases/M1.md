## Found and fixed during these checks

- **Medical emergency sheet ran to two pages on US Letter** (Garcia household):
  tighter print margins and spacing; all sheets now fit on one page, A4 and Letter.
- **The first home page did not say what to do first** (Lane B, J1): it now shows
  *Start here: Add a few things from the pantry* until something is added, and
  "No water recorded yet" in place of "About 0 days of water".
- **Expiring items could be hidden from the home summary** (Lane B, J5): the home
  card now shows at least one running-low item, one use-by date and one gap.
- **Wording** (Lane B's module review): the two emergency sheets now say which is
  the fridge copy and which has private medical details; water amounts say
  "enter litres, not bottles"; phone numbers come first on a person's form; the
  supplies sheet says which kinds of stock it covers.

## For Corey: the three checks that need a person

Use a Windows 10 or 11 computer that has never had Python installed, and a USB stick.

1. **First start.** Download the zip as the README describes, unzip it to the
   USB stick and double-click *Open Dashboard*. Expect the "Windows protected
   your PC" box; *More info*, *Run anyway*. The welcome page should open in the
   browser. Note anything that asks for administrator rights (nothing should).
2. **Twenty items.** Ask someone who has not seen it to add 20 things from the
   pantry, starting from *Add to Stock items* on the home page, and time it.
   Under ten minutes passes. Note where they hesitated.
3. **Unplug.** Click *Close safely*, wait for "safe to unplug", pull the stick
   out, plug it into the same or another computer, open it again and check the
   20 items are all there.

While it is open, print the emergency sheet on a real printer and check it is
one page.
