# Ideas we build on

Good ideas come from somewhere. This page names the books and thinkers whose
ideas shape Dashboard Plus, says in our own words what we took from each, and
shows where it appears in the product.

Dashboard Plus is an independent project. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or sponsored by** any author or publisher named here. We have not copied
their text; the descriptions below are our own summaries of ideas. If you find
an idea valuable, please buy and read the original.

## Atomic Habits: every action is a small vote

- **Source:** *Atomic Habits* by James Clear (Avery, 2018, ISBN 9780735211292).
  The author's site: <https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits>
- **The idea, in our words:** each small action is a vote for the kind of person
  you want to be. No single vote decides anything, but the votes add up, and
  the tally becomes evidence of who you are. Choose the vote you want to cast.
- **Where it shows up here:**
  - Entering a receipt, rotating the water, logging a weigh-in or checking an
    expiry date is a small vote for "a household that looks after what it has".
  - Entry is kept quick, because small, easy actions are the ones that repeat.
  - The monthly "What your records did for you" screen shows the votes adding
    up: waste down, no shortages, something caught early. It celebrates
    progress and never shames a missed day.

## Prepared: think it through before it happens

- **Source:** *Prepared: A Manual for Surviving Worst-Case Scenarios* by Mike
  Glover (Portfolio/Penguin, 2023, ISBN 9780593538142).
- **The idea, in our words:** preparedness starts in the mind. Work through a
  situation before it happens, while you are calm, so you are not meeting it
  for the first time in a crisis.
- **Where it shows up here:** the habit of asking "what if?" about your own
  home. Do I know the way out of each room? If I am stuck upstairs, can I get
  down? Do I know where the water is, and how long it lasts? Stock, People and
  the emergency sheet exist so those answers are written down and found fast.
  A "What if?" rehearsal feature (scenario checklists you answer and revisit
  on a schedule) is a likely future module; see `docs/VISION.md`.

## How we credit ideas (for contributors)

1. Name the work, author, publisher and year, and link to the author's or
   publisher's own page. Do not use cover images or logos.
2. Explain the idea in your own words. Keep any direct quotation to a short
   phrase, clearly marked and attributed. Never paste passages.
3. Say "inspired by" or "builds on", never "based on" or "official". Never
   suggest the author has approved the project.
4. Do not use a work's title, or a person's name, as the name of a feature or
   of the product.
5. Add the entry here, with where the idea appears in the product, and record
   any change of direction it causes in `docs/decisions/`.
6. If an author or publisher asks for a change, make it promptly.
