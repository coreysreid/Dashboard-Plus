They can inspire questions about household water, communications, equipment and practice. They are **not** the source for emergency phone numbers, local hazards, medical advice, water quantities or a claim that a household is safe. Use the official sources in the [AU](country-packs/AU.md) and [US](country-packs/US.md) notes for those facts, and check the latest advice for a particular address. A link here is not an endorsement, affiliation or product recommendation.

## Questions worth checking with public authorities

- **Water:** Cana's [planning worksheet](https://cana-provisions.com/products/water-plan-worksheets) and [water-quality hub](https://cana-provisions.com/pages/know-your-water) suggest checking whether a household knows where its stored water is, when it was last rotated, and which source it would use if the usual supply stopped. Use the official water guidance in the country notes for quantities and treatment claims.
- **Communication:** Spiritus Systems' [radio-equipment section](https://www.spiritussystems.com/shop/pouches/radio-pouches/) and the Radio Made Easy lead suggest asking how a household would contact people during a phone or power outage. Verify any legal, technical or emergency-radio instruction with the relevant public communications authority before writing a prompt.
- **Practice:** The training pages from [Rōnin Tactics](https://ronintactics.com/events--training.html) and [Dynamis Alliance](https://crusheverything.com/training/) suggest asking whether the household has practiced its own plan. The product should not reproduce a course or imply that buying training or gear proves readiness.
