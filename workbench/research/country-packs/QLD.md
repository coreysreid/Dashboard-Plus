# Batch 21 item 101: South East Queensland source handoff

Checked 21 November 2026. This is source research for a **Queensland** row within a future Australian country pack, not a live warning feed or a claim that a hazard applies to User 1's address. The official pages below were opened during this check. Direct command-line fetches of the QFD and SES pages returned a short site response despite HTTP 200, so a future pack editor should re-open those pages in a browser before publishing their exact wording. This handoff paraphrases facts and links to the authorities; it reproduces no maps, logos or instruction blocks.

## Proposed sourced rows

Each row would carry `country = "AU"`, `region = "QLD"`, authority, topic, official URL, `checked = "2026-11-21"`, a maintenance `review_by` date, licence/reuse status, the linked question and drill IDs, and a short attributed sentence. Dynamic alerts are links with `live = true`, never stored as a current status. Queensland is a jurisdiction label, not a household hazard decision. The household still chooses its applicable hazards and its local council or service.

## Pack boundary and wording

The pack should say: **“According to Queensland SES (checked 21/11/26), its emergency-kit guidance uses 10 litres of water per person for three days. Check your water provider and local advice for current conditions.”** This is a sourced kit starting point, not a stock calculation that certifies enough water. The current draft `AU.toml` cites NSW Rural Fire Service for **3 litres per person per day**. Keep both source, geography and unit period visible; never label the NSW figure Queensland guidance.

For warnings, use: **“Open current Queensland warnings”** or **“Check your water provider's current notice.”** Do not present a saved warning as current. For fire and cyclone drills, keep **“This is practice for a calm moment”** and defer to current authority and building instructions. A QLD pack row adds a local link and source date; it does not change a Yes answer or automatically tick a hazard in Settings.
