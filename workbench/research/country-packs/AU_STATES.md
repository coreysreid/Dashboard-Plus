## Reuse and promotion limits

- [ACT Government copyright](https://www.act.gov.au/copyright) and [NSW Government copyright](https://www.nsw.gov.au/nsw-government/about-website/copyright) generally allow CC BY 4.0 reuse of their own site material with exceptions. [NSW SES](https://www.ses.nsw.gov.au/privacy-copyright-disclaimer) states CC BY 4.0 for its site material with exceptions. The [NSW RFS copyright page](https://www.rfs.nsw.gov.au/about-us/privacy) also states CC BY 4.0 but later restricts retransmission and commercialisation; resolve that internal tension before reusing its text. Attribute any licensed reuse and check the exact page's exclusions; a state licence does not automatically cover a different agency domain.
- [SA Government](https://www.sa.gov.au/topics/emergencies-and-safety) states CC BY 4.0 on its own site. The [NT Government terms](https://nt.gov.au/page/copyright-disclaimer-and-privacy), [Tasmanian Government terms](https://www.tas.gov.au/codi) and [WA Government terms](https://www.wa.gov.au/terms-of-use#intellectual-property-rights) do not grant a blanket licence to adapt and republish all linked agency material. [NT Police, Fire and Emergency Services](https://pfes.nt.gov.au/node/849) separately asserts copyright. Recheck terms for ESA, CFS, SES, TFS, CFA, DFES, health pages and every utility before reusing text or images. This draft uses links and original factual paraphrases only; no maps, logos, live feed content or instruction blocks are copied.
- Dynamic warnings, water notices and power outages are **links**, never a saved status. A pack must show the source, jurisdiction and checked date for static guidance, but a checked date does not make a warning current. A household chooses its applicable local hazards, building instructions and providers. None of these rows changes a Readiness answer, claims that a recorded exit is safe, or certifies water as drinkable.
- This is not loadable pack data. The current country-pack contract still needs versioned subregion rows, per-fact source/review dates and a live-link flag, as [QLD.md](QLD.md) explains. Before promotion, verify the SA Health water-alert destination, the selected provider links for each locality and every agency's reuse terms. Keep the existing NSW RFS water figure explicitly NSW-sourced; do not present it as advice from the other six jurisdictions.
