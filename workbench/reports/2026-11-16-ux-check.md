# UX check after the catalogue draft merge

Checked **2026-11-16** against `main` at `dfcda8c` (after catalogue PR #22 merged). Used the three synthetic households with `python tools/load_example.py <household> --data <temporary folder>`, plus a fresh empty household. Opened the same local web routes a person uses for J1–J5; made stock changes only in temporary copies. No real household data was used. The catalogue is still a workbench draft, so its picker is not expected to appear yet.

## Findings, worst first

### 1. Important — flushing water raises the drinking-water estimate (J3)

**Did:** Sam Taylor's example initially showed **12 litres** and **about 4 days** for one person. Added a synthetic Stock item named **Flushing water**, category **Water**, amount **20 litres**, and opened *Needs attention*.

**Expected:** Water set aside for flushing would stay out of days of **drinking** water. A household should be able to see the basis of the count.

**Happened:** The page showed **32 litres on hand**, implying about **10.7 days**, because every item in the Water category counts. There is no drinking-water flag. The other two examples contain drinking water only, so this error is hidden in their ordinary view.

**Suggested wording and behavior:** On the home card: “About 4 days of **drinking water**. Counted: 12 litres marked for drinking. Not counted: 20 litres for washing or flushing.” In the Stock form: “Count this as drinking water? Only choose Yes if this water is safe to drink now.” The core must filter the supply total on a confirmed potable flag; the catalogue draft describes the source-backed defaults and exceptions. Do not change the wording alone while the sum is wrong.

### 2. Important — Stock in still begins with a long item dropdown (J2)

**Did:** Opened *Stock in and out* for the Smith family (26 stock items), Garcia household (24) and Sam Taylor (11), then looked for Kidney beans by typing the first letters.

**Expected:** As J2 says, type the first letters, see a short list, choose the good or add it if missing.

**Happened:** The Item control is a full alphabetical dropdown of the household's existing items. A missing name has no “add it” path in this form; the person must leave Stock in and add an item elsewhere. In the Smith example, there are 26 options before the other fields.

**Suggested wording and behavior:** Label it “Find an item”; placeholder “Type a name, such as kidney beans”; after matches, “Add ‘Kidney beans’ as a new item”. If an existing item is selected, show its unit and current amount before asking “How many did you bring in?” The merged catalogue draft gives matching and local-addition rules, but a core picker is still needed.

### 3. Minor — use-by dates read as ISO in lists and reminders (J5)

**Did:** Opened Stock items and *Needs attention* for the Australian Smiths, US Garcias and UK Sam Taylor.

**Expected:** Dates displayed and typed in the household's familiar order, with the format clear in the form.

**Happened:** The Smith Stock list showed dates such as `2027-04-25`; Sam's reminder said “Oat milk: expires 2026-11-22 (in 6 days)”. These are unambiguous but feel unlike the requested AU/UK and US forms.

**Suggested wording and behavior:** In the Smith list, “25/04/27”; for Sam, “Oat milk: use by 22/11/26 (in 6 days)”. In the Garcias' US view, show month first, for example “04/25/27”. Give the input a visible format hint and store ISO internally. The stock-lots design specifies parsing and display needs.
