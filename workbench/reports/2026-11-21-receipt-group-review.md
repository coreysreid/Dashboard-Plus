## Findings, worst first

1. **Important — the grouped action still needs exact consequences on each button.** On Review, the group says, “These 2 rows go together: accepting a row also adds the new record it needs, and rejecting a new record also rejects the rows that need it.” The new **Stock item** row has an **Accept** button, but accepting that row alone leaves its Stock in waiting; only accepting the dependent Stock in or **Accept all** adds both. The row-specific “Accepting this also adds the new record above that it needs” appears only on the movement. A person can read “go together” as “either Accept adds both.” **Replace the group text:** “Accepting this Stock in also adds its new Stock item. Accepting only the item leaves Stock in waiting. Rejecting the item rejects its Stock in too.” Label the movement button **“Accept Stock in and new item”** when it has that dependency. This requires Review to render dependency-specific action words; no data-model change is needed. In this run, **Accept all 4** did add the linked pair correctly, and rejecting the second item rejected its movement.
2. **Important — a bought amount lacks its unit at the decision point.** The new-item movement reads “Item: Demo red lentils (new, suggested above) · Reason: Bought or added · Amount to add or use: 1 · Date: 20/11/26.” Its item row says “Unit: packs”; the receipt source says `500G`. The person has to combine three rows of evidence to see that this means **one pack**, not one gram or one kilogram. **Replace that value phrase on a bought movement:** “Add 1 pack of Demo red lentils on 20/11/26.” For an existing item, use that item's saved unit, for example “Add 2 tins of Kidney beans on 20/11/26.” Keep “500G” in the source line as package size only. Core needs a read-only Review formatter that resolves a proposed item's unit through `{"ref": 2}`; the stored movement values and amount stay unchanged.
3. **Minor — the success line is too broad after a mixed batch.** **Accept all 4** returns “Added 4 records.” The four are three Stock in entries and one new Stock item; the group disappears, leaving no immediate summary of what the new item now holds. **Replace with:** “Added 3 Stock in entries and 1 new Stock item. Kidney beans now has 15 tins; Tinned tomatoes has 4 tins; Demo red lentils has 1 pack.” This is an example of generated words, not a fixed line; core would need to return the created record types and resulting counts. A shorter safe alternative is “Added 4 records, including a new Stock item and its first Stock in. Check Household stock for the amounts.”

The source line and price display worked at both widths, with zero document overflow. The same source appears on both the new item and its movement; it is repetitive, but it keeps the source next to either decision. The form says “Shown for checking only; it is not saved in your records.” That is accurate for Stock records: the source is retained with the Review proposal, not copied into a Stock item, batch or movement. No source price was saved as Stock price. No blocker was found in the linked add/reject behavior.

## Exact run and result

The helper was **Example receipt helper**, granted Look and suggest for Household stock only. The connector answered `dashboard_me`, `dashboard_tables` and `dashboard_list_records` before it suggested anything. In this fresh fixture the listed Kidney beans item was ID 6, 13 tins, and Tinned tomatoes was ID 5, 1 tin; the script read these IDs from `record.values` instead of assuming them. The key was supplied only to the connector process and omitted from logs and tools.

The first `dashboard_suggest_new_records` request used one new `request_key`, a short invented reason and these four entries (the order matters):

```json
[
  {"table":"stock__movements","values":{"item":6,"reason":"bought","amount":2,"happened_on":"2026-11-20"},"source":{"line":"KIDNEY BEANS 400G / 2 x $1.20","price":"$2.40"}},
  {"table":"stock__movements","values":{"item":5,"reason":"bought","amount":3,"happened_on":"2026-11-20"},"source":{"line":"TINNED TOMATOES 400G / 3 x $1.10","price":"$3.30"}},
  {"table":"stock__items","values":{"name":"Demo red lentils","category":"pantry","unit":"packs"},"source":{"line":"DEMO RED LENTILS 500G x1","price":"$2.10"}},
  {"table":"stock__movements","values":{"item":{"ref":2},"reason":"bought","amount":1,"happened_on":"2026-11-20"},"source":{"line":"DEMO RED LENTILS 500G x1","price":"$2.10"}}
]
```

The connector replied **“4 suggestions are waiting for the household to review. Nothing changes until they accept them.”** The new movement reported `depends_on: [2]`. The script initially included `quantity: 0` on the new Stock item; the API correctly refused the entire first attempt with “is worked out from its batches; suggest a Stock in instead.” Removing that computed field made the request valid. There was no partial save.

A second request used two entries: a new invented Demo chickpeas item at position 0, then a bought movement with `{"ref":0}`, both with the invented source line and $1.50. Before browser decisions, all Smith amounts were unchanged. Review showed both source lines, both linked groups, the new-item placeholder and the reject consequence at 320px and desktop. The page's document width equalled the viewport width (320/1280).

At 320px, **Accept all 4** returned “Added 4 records.” Kidney beans became **15 tins**, Tinned tomatoes **4 tins**, and Demo red lentils was created at **1 pack in one batch**. The first batch's four proposals were accepted. At desktop width, pressing **Reject** on Demo chickpeas returned “Rejected. Nothing was added to Stock items. 1 row that needed it was rejected too.” Both proposals in that second batch were rejected; no Demo chickpeas item or movement was created. Both batches then had `state: decided`, and Review had nothing waiting. The source line strings were absent from the created Stock movement notes. No inferred use-by date or place was added.
