# Integration and reuse notes

Checked 17 November 2026. The five checklists paraphrase and link official NSW RFS, Australian Bureau of Meteorology, Queensland Government, US National Weather Service, UK Met Office, US CDC and NOAA pages, with URLs beside each topic in CHECKLISTS.md. No text, diagrams or imagery are reproduced. NOAA, NWS and CDC federal works are generally public domain under [17 U.S.C. 105](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html), subject to third-party exceptions. The [Queensland Government copyright page](https://www.qld.gov.au/legal/copyright) generally gives CC BY 4.0 with exceptions. NSW RFS and Met Office reuse terms for these pages were not confirmed, so link and paraphrase only. Check source links and terms again before promotion; these pages can change.

The core needs a dated checklist-answer store, applicable-region and household-hazard rules, a person-selected local warning source, source/review dates, and record links that show full paths. Each check must remain a question even when a linked record exists. Store a checked date and reviewer separately from the record's own test, expiry or movement date. Do not create a universal “ready” score. The present Home layout module is still a workbench draft; Stock places are shipped and nested, but shared Home layout equipment/exits/shut-offs need promotion before those links work. A missing module or field should show “No linked record yet”, never a silent complete state.

The only fixed season dates in this draft are attributed to BOM's Australian tropical cyclone season (1 Nov–30 Apr) and NOAA's Atlantic/central Pacific hurricane season (1 Jun–30 Nov). Bushfire, winter-storm and heat timing comes from the household and current local authority; the program must not infer a month from country alone. The questions do not prescribe medical care, operation of utilities or a specific amount of emergency stock.
