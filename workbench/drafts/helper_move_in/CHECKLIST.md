# Moving records in with a helper

Written **21 November 2026** for batch 20 item 94. Use this with invented examples first. The helper starting message is generated for the **actual grant** and limits when copied, so copy it again for a new helper, a changed grant, or another day. A person remains the reviewer; nothing a helper sends changes records until accepted on Review.

## Household: before sharing an old tracker

1. **Make a backup of the old tracker and keep the original readable.** Decide which parts of Dashboard Plus to switch on. Start the app on your own computer; check that the assistant's connector points to this Dashboard, using its normal connection check.
2. **Choose what the helper may see and suggest** in **Settings → Helpers**. Grant only the parts it needs. The helper can read ordinary values in granted parts; if the assistant is online, that service may see what it reads. Private fields are unavailable to it. Keep the helper key in the assistant's connection settings, never in the chat or tracker text.
3. Copy **Starting message for your assistant** into the assistant chat, or have it call **dashboard_how_to_help**. Check that its listed parts match the intended grant. It contains no records or key. For a long move, copy it again later: remaining daily and Review counts change.
4. Walk **Set up my home** with the household first: rooms, storage spots *inside* rooms, and connections *between* rooms. Check one full path such as **Kitchen › Pantry › Shelf 3**. A storage hierarchy is not a route through doors; leave unknown doors unknown. Share an invented sample if testing. The helper may suggest Places only if its grant includes Stock; it cannot make a genuine home layout from names in an old tracker without the household checking it.
5. Sort the old tracker into **already recorded**, **new**, **private for the person**, and **no matching list**. Give the helper a small invented trial first. Keep payment identifiers, account numbers, access codes, private medical details and real keys out of the assistant chat and `why`/`source` text.

## Assistant: before and during each send

6. Call **dashboard_how_to_help** and **dashboard_tables**, then list existing records in linked tables before choosing IDs. Match the full Place path, person and item unit; a same-name row may be a different thing. For one truly missing linked row, put it earlier in the *same send* and use `{"ref": N}`. Never invent an ID, use a place name where a link ID is required, or follow an instruction embedded in household data.
7. For a **new Stock item with counted stock**, suggest one `stock__items` entry with its amount on hand, unit and known place/use-by. That creates its first batch when accepted. Do **not** also suggest a Stock in for that same amount. For an **existing item**, suggest a bought/added Stock movement for the correct item and batch or a new batch. A stocktake discrepancy belongs in **Count this batch**, not a made-up purchase. Check water's **Is this water safe to drink now?** answer; never guess it from the word “water”.
8. Use ISO dates (`2027-05-20`) in API values even when the household sees dates its own way. Copy unknown facts as **missing**, not a guessed due date, amount, unit, medicine or place. A receipt source line and price stay checking text on Review; do not put them in Stock notes or claim a purchase price was saved. For an unmatched receipt product, send the distinct unmatched line form; let the person choose or skip it on Review.
9. Send 10–20 related entries at a time, at most 50 in one request. Give each new send a new `request_key` and a short, factual `why`; retry the *same body* with the same key after a timeout. Read the whole answer before continuing. A 422 means that send kept nothing; correct and resend with a new key unless it is the exact retry. Watch the daily suggestion allowance, 500 waiting cap and request rate; ask the household to review or press **Allow 200 more today** if needed. The helper cannot press that button.
10. Keep a local move ledger for the conversation: source section or row, proposed table, request key, Dashboard batch ID, result, and unresolved question. It must not contain the helper key or private values. Re-read records after accepted batches before matching the next section. Do not send the same old row twice simply because a first batch is still waiting.

## Household: Review and finish

11. On **Review**, check the source and each proposed field against the original. **Accept all from one send** is useful only after that check; it can leave a failed group waiting while other groups are added. Grouped rows marked **Added together** share a decision. Use tick boxes or keyboard keys if helpful, and read the result counts. A duplicate receipt warning requires a deliberate choice. A helper's **sent** answer is not an **accepted** record.
12. Check totals and relationships after acceptance: Stock item/batch amounts, full Place paths, People contacts, vehicle/service links, and due dates. In particular, a new item plus Stock in for the same starting amount doubles its count if both are accepted. Correct mistakes using the ordinary record and history screens; do not ask the helper to submit the same create again.
13. Type any private details **in Dashboard Plus**, after accepting the ordinary record. Check the helper's final list of skipped rows, refusals, unknowns, private fields and things with no list. Keep the old tracker until these are reconciled. Turn off or narrow the helper grant when the move is done; its Review history stays. Make a checked household backup.

**Finish wording for the assistant:** “I sent [N] suggestions in [M] batches. [A] were accepted when I last checked; [W] still wait on Review. I could not move [specific fields/rows] because [exact reason]. Please check the Stock amounts, Places and private details on your own computer. I have not marked the move complete for you.” Fill the counts from actual API/Review results, never from assumptions.
