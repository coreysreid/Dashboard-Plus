# Vehicles draft

A household can name a vehicle, record its registration reminder, keep insurance and service reminders, link a roadside assistance contact stored in People, and point to a car emergency kit tracked in Household stock. The three watch rules use `wording = "due"` because these are tasks or renewals, not food expiry. A date is optional; no default legal or service interval is asserted. The household should use its own notice, policy and vehicle schedule. The example dates, numbers and provider names are fictional.

**People and kit hand-off:** The sample `example.toml` includes synthetic People contacts with fiction-reserved phone numbers and the existing `other` role, plus one Stock item at a Car boot or trunk place for each household. Lane A should load those supporting rows before linking each Vehicle. The People module has no `roadside_assistance` role yet; add that role when promoting Vehicles so the contact is easy to find under Who to call. The current declarative link can point to any People row and cannot enforce its role or a nonblank phone; Lane A needs that validation or a clear prompt. Keep the number in People so changes appear once everywhere. Stock's kit is a real item with amount and place; the Vehicle link does not create kit contents or change its quantity.

**Core and migration needs:** The current `watch` contract permits one expiring date per table, so registration, insurance and services have separate tables. Lane A can promote them without inventing multiple-date watch syntax. Date entry and display still need the household-country handling in Stock lots `ACCEPTANCE.md` so AU/UK/NZ see day first and the US sees month first. A future Home layout promotion must migrate `parked_at` and the kit's Stock place link with a backup and a way back; a dangling place must never be displayed as if it still exists. Show registration numbers only on an opened record or an explicitly chosen export; this draft marks them sensitive and omits them from the list. Do not put registration or policy numbers on a printable sheet by default.

Checked against shipped People roles, Stock Places and items, Pets-style date watches, and `docs/DESIGN.md` sections 5 and 6 on **2026-11-17**. No legal renewal period or vehicle safety instruction is taken from an external source; the draft contains only household-entered reminders.
