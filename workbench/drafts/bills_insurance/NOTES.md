# Ownership, privacy and promotion gaps

Checked **21 November 2026**: shipped `modules/utilities/` (decision 0099), `modules/vehicles/`, `modules/people/`, `docs/MODULES.md`, decisions 0089 and 0100, and `docs/DESIGN.md` §§5–6. These are product and implementation sources. The draft gives no insurer, payment, coverage, tax or legal advice. A household must check its current bill and policy for the actual amount, due date, cover and exclusions. The dates in `example.toml` and its providers are invented.

**Place bills beside Utilities, not inside it.** Utilities owns a service, fault contact, meter and local-only account note. A bill is a dated financial commitment, which may cover that service or a vehicle, pet, home or person. Adding amount/frequency/renewal fields to Utilities would leave insurance without a home and confuse a service contact with a payment. Keep its meter and fault line in Utilities; this draft stores only a broad bill name and optional Place. A future exact `utilities.services` link would avoid duplicate provider entry for an electricity bill, but would make Utilities mandatory for every policy under today's `requires` contract. That optional dependency/link is core work.

**Vehicles already owns vehicle insurance renewal.** `vehicles.insurance` has vehicle, name, `renew_by`, notes and a 30-day reminder. Do not promote both independent reminders without a migration and household choice. The target should have one authoritative vehicle-policy row, mapped by existing Vehicles insurance ID to the new commitment and preserving the user-entered renewal date; duplicate names alone are not safe matching. Until that exists, keep Vehicles' reminder authoritative and this draft disabled for live households. Notes in Vehicles may hold details that should remain local; do not copy them automatically. No shipped record or migration is edited here.

**Private fields and travel:** `amount`, `covers`, `reference` and `notes` are `sensitive = true, travels = "never"`. They stay on the computer where entered, out of the portable log values; decision 0100's backup still keeps them locally. `name`, `kind`, `category`, `provider`, `frequency`, `next_due`, `vehicle` and `place` are ordinary and may travel under 0089. A name or Place can still disclose a household fact; labels ask for broad names, and helper permissions should remain explicit. Do not put a private field in `record_name`, a watch message or a sheet. Private does **not** mean encrypted storage. Whether household members later need amounts across devices is a product decision requiring a new travel rule and migration review, not an implicit default.

**Core and draft limits:** The present module contract requires all named dependencies. This draft's vehicle link makes Vehicles mandatory even for a household with only an electricity bill; promotion needs an optional vehicle link or split extension. No cross-module exact utility-service link is available without making Utilities mandatory. `expiring` can warn before a person-entered `next_due`, but cannot represent paid/renewed history or advance a date from frequency. Do not infer a bill has been paid or a policy is active. The current sheet renderer has a 200-row section limit; a large household needs continuation or a refusal before a complete printout is promised. Confirm `money` display uses the household currency and the household's date order before promotion.
