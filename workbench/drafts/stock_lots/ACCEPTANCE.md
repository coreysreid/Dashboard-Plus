# Stock lots and household dates: acceptance checks

Checked against the [lots design](NOTES.md), shipped Stock fields and `docs/DESIGN.md` section 5 on **2026-11-17**. These numbered checks describe what Lane A can test when lots and local date entry ship. Use a fixed household date of **20 April 2027** where the wording says “in 30 days”. Start each check from its stated fixture so earlier actions do not change later expectations. All names and amounts below are made up.

## One starting item

Create **Kidney beans**, Pantry, unit **tins**, **Keep at least 6**. Unless a check says otherwise, it has a lot of **3 tins**, use by **2027-05-20**, at **Kitchen › Pantry › Shelf 3**, and another of **10 tins**, use by **2027-11-20**, at **Garage › Grey shelves**. The item list says **13 tins on hand**. The item has one identity; each lot owns its place, amount and date. No single editable item quantity may disagree with the sum of active lots.

## Numbered checks

1. **A stocktake sets the counted amount.** Start with only the 3-tin May lot. Type `kid`, choose Kidney beans, choose Kitchen › Pantry › Shelf 3, enter **5 tins** and **20/05/27** in an AU household. The form says **“3 tins recorded here. You counted 5. Update this lot to 5?”** Choosing **Update count** leaves one lot of 5, an item total of 5, and an auditable **+2 stocktake correction**. It must not add five to make eight. Cancel leaves three. A different date or place offers **Add a separate lot** and leaves the original lot untouched.
2. **A weekly shop adds stock to the chosen lot.** Start with only the 3-tin May lot. In Stock in, find Kidney beans, enter **10 tins**, use by **20/11/27**, at Garage › Grey shelves, and choose **Bought or added**. A new November lot holds 10 and the total becomes 13. Say **“Added 10 tins. Kidney beans: 13 tins on hand.”** If a lot already has that exact item, place and date, offer **“Add to this lot”** with its current amount shown; adding must be an explicit choice. Two date stamps on one receipt create two lots.
3. **Using stock suggests the oldest known date first.** Start from the two-lot fixture. In Stock out, enter **2 tins used**. Preselect the May lot and show **“Use 2 tins from the 3 tins with the earliest use-by date: Kitchen › Pantry › Shelf 3, use by 20/05/27?”** The person can choose the November lot instead. Confirming May leaves 1 + 10 = 11 tins, with one movement linked to the May lot. An undated lot must be shown as **“Date not recorded”**, not assumed newest or fresh, and must not outrank a known earlier date by default. Warn before an amount spans lots and show the split for approval.
4. **A lot cannot go below zero.** Start from the fixture and try to use 4 tins from the 3-tin May lot. Refuse Save with **“Only 3 tins are in this lot. Choose another lot or split the amount.”** The two lots and item total remain unchanged. A lot for another item cannot be selected through a crafted request.
5. **Throwing out an expired lot keeps its history.** Use a fixed date of **21 May 2027** with the two-lot fixture. Open the May lot, choose **Throw away**, and confirm **3 tins** with reason **Expired**. The May lot becomes zero or archived but stays in history; the November lot remains 10 and the item total becomes 10. The discard is one auditable movement, not a deletion of old records. Do not automatically call a food unsafe merely because a best-before date passed.
6. **Needs attention speaks about each positive lot.** On 20 April 2027, the starting fixture shows **“3 tins of Kidney beans expire in 30 days”** linked to the May lot and its full path. The November lot does not appear yet. On 21 May 2027, if the May lot still has 3 tins, show **“3 tins of Kidney beans expired on 20/05/27”** in an AU household. After check 5, the zero May lot no longer warns. If two different lots are within 30 days, both dates and amounts remain visible; do not merge them into a misleading single date.
7. **Running low uses the total.** With 3 + 10 tins and minimum 6, Kidney beans is not Running low. After using 8 across confirmed lots, the total is 5 and the list says **“Kidney beans: 5 tins on hand; keep at least 6.”** Restoring or correcting a lot back to 6 removes the low notice. The Stock list, home card and sheets agree on the total after every action.
8. **AU, UK and NZ type and see day first.** Set each household country in turn and type **20/05/27** for the lot. Save as ISO **2027-05-20** internally. Reopen the form, Stock list, lot page, Needs attention and printable sheet: each displays **20/05/27**. The input hint says **“Day / month / year (DD/MM/YY)”**. Typing **1/2/27** previews **“1 February 2027”** and saves 2027-02-01; display it as **01/02/27**. NZ follows the same rule as AU and UK.
9. **US types and sees month first.** Set country US. Type **05/20/27** and store 2027-05-20. The same five surfaces show **05/20/27** and the hint says **“Month / day / year (MM/DD/YY)”**. Typing **1/2/27** previews **“January 2, 2027”** and stores 2027-01-02; display **01/02/27**. A household changing its country changes presentation, not stored dates or lot identity.
10. **ISO and two-digit years are deliberate.** Typing **2027-05-20** is accepted as an unambiguous ISO date in any country and redisplayed in the household format. Typing year **27** means **2027**, never 1927. Before Save, show the expanded long-date preview; the core must document a consistent rule for all two-digit years. Proposed rule: `00` through `99` mean 2000 through 2099. A four-digit year remains available for dates outside that range.
11. **Invalid dates never roll over.** In AU/UK/NZ, reject **31/04/27** and **29/02/27**; in the US, reject **02/30/27**. Say **“Enter a real date in DD/MM/YY format”** or **“Enter a real date in MM/DD/YY format”** as appropriate, keep everything else the person entered, and save nothing. **29/02/28** in AU/UK/NZ and **02/29/28** in the US are valid leap-day examples. An optional date can be left blank and displays **“Date not recorded.”**
12. **A current single-date item becomes one lot.** Upgrade a shipped Stock item with quantity 5, unit tins, minimum 6, place Kitchen › Pantry › Shelf 3 and `expires = 2027-05-20`. It becomes one lot of 5 with that date and linked place; the item total is 5 and Running low still applies. Preserve the item ID, name, category, notes and existing movement history; do not replay old movements to add or subtract the five again. An item with no date gets an undated lot. An item with zero amount gets no positive lot or a zero archived lot, but never an expiry warning. If migration fails, restore from a backup or roll the transaction back so the old item and links remain usable.
13. **Lots carry the water decision and unit correctly.** For a Water item split across lots, only confirmed drinkable litres enter days of drinking water; a flushing-water lot and tablets add zero. A quantity recorded as bottles cannot be summed as litres without the household confirming litres per bottle. This preserves the J3 basis while Stock moves from one item amount to lots.

## Core work this requires

Lane A needs lot records, a derived item amount, atomic stocktake corrections and movements linked to a specific lot; a lot picker that suggests the earliest known date but allows another choice; per-lot expiry watches and summed-item running-low watches; a reversible migration of current single-date items; and one country-aware date parser and formatter used across forms, lists, reminders and sheets. These are core changes, not rules the draft TOML can enforce today. The [design notes](NOTES.md) give the screen flow and migration detail.
