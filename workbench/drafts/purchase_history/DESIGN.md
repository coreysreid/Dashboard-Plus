# Purchase and price history for Stock

Designed **21 November 2026** for batch 20 item 92. This is a core/Stock design, not a loadable draft module: a purchase must be linked transactionally to the Stock in movement that it describes. No shipped table or Review route is changed here.

## What is recorded

A `purchase` is one shopping trip or receipt, with a stable ID, purchase date, optional shop label, currency, optional **receipt total actually paid**, and whether all lines are accounted for. A `purchase_line` identifies one bought Stock in movement by permanent ID, its item and batch at acceptance, the movement's amount/unit, optional **line total actually paid**, and whether tax/discount is included in that line total. The Stock movement remains the sole count change. The purchase line is evidence about its cost, never another movement. A person may record a Stock in without any price or shop; it still counts as stock, with cost unknown.

Receipt total and line totals are **different facts**. Never add both. A receipt may contain goods outside Stock, fees, tax, a multi-buy discount, a refund or a card payment. If line totals do not reconcile to the receipt total, say **Price details incomplete** and preserve both, rather than allocate the difference silently. A receipt line with `2 tins` and `3.80` might mean a line total or a unit price; the helper's `source.price` from decision 0097 is checking text only. The household must say **Total for this line** or **Price for each unit** before a value becomes a Stock purchase fact; if unclear, leave it blank. Unit-price history can divide a confirmed line total by a positive bought amount only when the item's unit is the same and the person confirmed that price basis. A pack/tin mismatch cannot be guessed from a catalogue size.

Use the accepted receipt group ID from decision 0097 and the possible-repeat decision in the receipt-entry draft to connect lines. `{"ref": 0}` may add a new item before its Stock in; the purchase line points to the resulting permanent movement ID after Accept, never to proposal position 0. A skipped/unmatched line has no movement and no item price. If a person later chooses an item for that line, attach the accepted movement then. Rejecting a Stock in creates no purchase line. Undoing a movement marks its cost line inactive for current totals but keeps the historical decision. A returned/refunded purchase needs an explicit refund event linked to the original; Stock out alone is not a refund.

For a hand-entered Stock in, the form can ask an optional shop and paid price. Saving creates the movement plus purchase metadata atomically; a failed movement creates neither. For a multi-line receipt, one purchase holds all accepted bought movements. A person can correct its shop/price with a revision and reason, without changing Stock amounts. A helper may send checking text on Review, but a permanent price is saved only after a person confirms the interpretation. The helper API's current add-only Stock suggestion does not include these financial fields.

## What a cost answer can say

Question: **What is preparing costing us?** A first answer can show **Recorded purchases for selected preparation items, [date range]: 124.50 [household currency] across 8 confirmed purchase lines. 3 bought lines have no confirmed price; 2 receipts include other goods, so this is not a full household cost.** Drill down to each receipt/movement and its price basis. The household chooses which Stock categories or item IDs count as “preparing”; do not assume all food, pet supplies or a generator purchase is preparedness. Do not treat today's on-hand value, catalogue typical prices, an estimate or a note as money spent. Show refunds separately and subtract only linked confirmed refunds. A month with missing prices is **incomplete**, never zero. No currency conversion or inflation adjustment is inferred.

## Privacy and travel

Actual amounts paid, shop, receipt total, line price, discount/tax detail and the purchase-to-movement relationship can reveal routines and finances. Propose these as **sensitive, local-only** in decision 0100 terms until Corey chooses a member-sharing policy. Item, quantity and Stock in date continue to travel as ordinary Stock facts. A paired member may see the movement but **Price recorded only on the computer where it was entered**. A member-entered price would remain on that member when the movement is approved on main; the cost question on main must mark its coverage incomplete rather than imply that it knows that price. Core needs a policy for deliberately transferring approved purchase evidence before promising cross-device cost answers. Raw receipt image, card digits, loyalty IDs and payment account numbers are never required or retained.
