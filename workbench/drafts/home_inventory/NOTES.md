# Home inventory: promotion check

Rechecked **19 November 2026** against shipped Stock Places, the sheet renderer, helper/API and general export, `docs/DESIGN.md` §5.2 and decisions 0054, 0059, 0071, 0072, 0075 and 0078. The invented examples contain no real serials or purchase evidence. The [NAIC home inventory page](https://content.naic.org/consumer/home-inventory) was last checked **17 November 2026** for the earlier draft; its US general guidance is not an insurer's claim rule for AU, UK or NZ.

**Not ready for promotion as a complete claim inventory.** `belongings.room` already links to shared `stock.places`, so no Home layout owner migration is needed. A sheet section that explicitly lists the draft's sensitive Serial field now opens behind the private-sheet POST warning; the old note asking for a new print-confirmation flag is obsolete. Decision 0072's linked phone and 0075's one-step action do not solve a claim-list need and require no draft action.

**Sheet completeness is a core blocker.** The current renderer orders `order_by = "room"` by the raw place ID, not its full nested path, and it silently stops each section at 200 rows. The intended claim sheet needs grouping and sorting by the displayed full place path, repeated room headings, safe page breaks and an explicit continuation or refusal instead of a partial 200-row printout. Test a household with more than 200 belongings and moved places before describing the sheet as complete. The private warning does not solve truncation.

**Decide the privacy boundary.** Serial and Notes are sensitive and omitted from default reveal, helper reads and the general export; the selected claim sheet includes Serial only after the private-sheet warning. Room, item name, make/model, paid cost and receipt/photo location are currently ordinary fields. A helper granted Home inventory and an ordinary export can see this map of valuables. Mark more fields sensitive if the household promise is a private inventory, then decide what the default list can still use for identification. The current general export omits Serial, so it is **not** a complete claim inventory; a complete private export would need an explicit, warned core option and a storage/handling decision. Backups keep all fields but are not a spreadsheet claim sheet. Do not imply that the data folder or exported file is encrypted.

**Evidence and money:** Receipt kept and Photos kept are descriptions of where evidence is, not uploaded attachments; avoid account paths, passwords and codes. Paid is the household's entered cost, never a current valuation, replacement price or promised payment. No exchange rate is assumed. Before promotion, resolve the sheet's completeness and the field/export privacy policy, then re-run the draft's acceptance checks with invented room paths and serials.

**Order:** Wine first, Utilities next after its links/phone sheet, Collections after value-total/privacy choices, and Home inventory last because the claim sheet and private export need core decisions.
