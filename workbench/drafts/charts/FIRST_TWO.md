Rechecked **21 November 2026** against shipped Stock's `supply` rule and `watch.supplies`, Pets weigh-ins, the Home water card, decision 0104 Readiness answers, and batch 20 item 92's *proposed* purchase history. The earlier [four chart sketches](CHARTS.md) remain ideas. These are the two most useful **first** charts because they can be built from accepted records already present, without inventing historical balances or paid prices. All example figures here are invented. Neither chart is a safety or health verdict.

## 1. Drinking water counted today

**Question:** “What makes up the days of drinking water shown on Home right now?”

**Chart:** One horizontal bar per current Stock water item measured in litres and answered **Yes, safe to drink now**, with the item's counted litres printed on the bar. A total line above says **24 L counted · about 2.4 days for 3 people and 1 pet · aim 3 days** (invented arithmetic: 3 L/person/day plus 1 L/pet/day, 10 L/day). Show the actual household count, basis, target and local **As recorded at [time]** from the same `watch.supplies` calculation as Home. Below, a separate **Left out of this calculation** list names water marked not drinking and items whose unit is bottles/packs or another non-litre unit; it does not put them in a zero-valued bar. A title and axis say **Litres counted now**, never “safe water guaranteed”.

**Exact data:** `stock.items` rows with `category = water`, `drinking = true`, positive current `quantity` worked out from `stock.lots`, and a unit accepted by the shipped `unit_words` rule. Use the same household person count fallback and pet count, 3 L per person plus 1 L per pet a day, and the rule's 3-day target. Read through one consistent database snapshot so the bars add to the Home total. Do not count a pending helper/member Stock in or an item with no answer to Safe to drink. Item and lot edits, deletion, undo and a changed People/Pets count recalculate on next view. **This chart is a current breakdown, not a line over time.** The old water-over-time sketch needs dated balances, correction history and household-size snapshots before it can be truthful.

**Empty and uncertain:** With no water items: **No drinking water recorded yet. Add litres and answer whether each water item is safe to drink now.** With items but no person count: **24 L counted. Tell Dashboard Plus how many people live here to work out days.** With only excluded items: **No litres are counted as drinking water yet. Check each item's answer and unit.** A zero amount is recorded zero; a missing amount is unknown, not zero. Keep the existing Home caveat: **These daily figures are a starting point. Check your local authority's advice.**

**Equal table:** Heading **How the current drinking-water estimate is worked out**. Columns: Item (link), full Place path (if recorded), current amount, saved unit, Safe to drink answer, counted litres or exact exclusion reason. Footer: counted total; people/pets and their source; daily basis; calculated days; chosen target; last record time. The table shows every bar value and every excluded item. On a phone, use row cards in the same order; labels and totals stay readable at 320 px. Sort by counted litres with a stable name/ID tie-breaker, never hide an excluded item because it does not fit the chart.

## 2. One pet's recorded weight

**Question:** “What weights have we written down for this pet, and when?”

**Chart:** One dot for each accepted `pets.weigh_ins` row of the selected pet, local date on the horizontal axis and kg on the vertical axis. Show **Luna · 3.8 kg on 20/11/26 · 2 weigh-ins recorded** (invented Garcia example). A single dot stays a single dot. With two or three widely spaced observations, do not draw a confident trend line; connecting dots can imply a measurement in between, so the default is dots only. A person can open the dated row or a separately recorded vet visit, but the chart never calls a visit, food change or medicine the cause of a weight change. The vertical scale shows the full plotted range and units; no truncated axis exaggerates a small change.

**Exact data:** `pets.weigh_ins.pet`, `day`, `weight` (kg) and permanent ID for all accepted, nondeleted rows of one pet within the chosen date range; sort by day then ID, including multiple readings on one day. `pets.vet_visits` may be shown as separately labelled dated markers only if the person opens that option, without copying private notes into the chart. Revisions, corrected dates and restored rows update both dot and table. Do not mix pets, convert units or infer weight at unmeasured dates. The prior draft's person-confirmed annotations need a new core record and are not part of this first chart.

**Empty and uncertain:** No pet: **Add a pet before showing its weight history.** Pet but no weigh-ins: **No weights recorded for Luna yet.** One reading: **One weight recorded. Add another dated reading to compare, if you choose to track this.** A date range with no points says **No weights recorded in this period**, not zero kg. Two or more points may be compared as recorded values, without diagnosis or a “healthy” band.

**Equal table:** Heading **Luna's recorded weights · [chosen date range]**. Columns: Date, weight in kg, record link and corrected-on marker if applicable; group same-day readings rather than average them without asking. A separate optional column links a vet visit on the date, explicitly **Recorded visit, not an explanation**. Every plotted dot has one row, including duplicate dates. Export/print the table with the date span and pet name; notes and microchip are absent. A keyboard user can select each dot and reach its matching row; a screen reader gets the table and text summary without needing the graphic.
