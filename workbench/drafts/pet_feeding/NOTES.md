# Core work and source boundary

Checked **21 November 2026** against shipped `modules/pets/`, the local helper OpenAPI description, decisions 0089 and 0100, and the proposed phone screens. These local sources establish record and Review contracts. No quantity or schedule here is veterinary advice; example food names and amounts are invented.

The draft module can hold individual events, but the core has no declared aggregate for household-local daily totals, no pet/day indexed filtered Home card, no date-range sheet with subtotals, and no complete-sheet pagination. Its current generic sheet stops at 200 rows, so no `sheets.toml` is provided. Add a query that sums only **accepted** measured entries by pet, local day and identical unit, separately reports unmeasured entries, and copes with late edits, timezone and daylight-saving changes. Add a bounded event list and an explicit complete print/export path. A `given_at` datetime currently lacks an event-specific timezone field; a durable instant plus IANA zone and household-local-day rule is needed before a phone in another zone can log reliably.

The existing helper API can suggest new records with a table grant and waits for Review; it cannot edit a mistaken event or establish cross-route duplicate identity. The proposed possible-repeat prompt, event correction, amount/unit pair validation and idempotent phone operations need core work. The member phone app and transfer are not shipped. Review and sync must not count a pending event as accepted.

`notes` is deliberately ordinary in this draft, so it may appear in helper reads or travel under decision 0100. The form warns against medical instructions and sensitive details. If households need private notes, change the field to `sensitive = true, travels = "never"` and confirm the product boundary before promotion. The pet name, food and bowl also reveal a routine to anyone granted the module; keep permission explicit and omit feeding details from OS notifications.
