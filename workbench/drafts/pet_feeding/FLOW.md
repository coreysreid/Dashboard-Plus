# Fast entry and honest daily totals

Designed **21 November 2026** for batch 20 item 89. The module files define event records only; the phone, daily-total, Home and sheet screens below need core work in [NOTES.md](NOTES.md). None is shipped yet.

**What one row means:** One deliberate addition of food to one pet's bowl, or one treat, at a local date and time. `kind = refill` means new food added, never the food already in the bowl. An optional amount is **put out**, not eaten. The row does not record what remained, calories, a diet target, or medical advice. A corrected mistaken entry edits the same event with a new revision; it does not create an offsetting negative serving. A pet can have many rows on one day.

**Phone member, one-hand path:** From **Pets → Rex → Add food**, show **Pet: Rex**, **When: Today, 7:15 am** prefilled from the phone's local clock with its timezone retained, **What happened: Bowl / Refill / Treat**, **Which bowl (optional)** with recent entries, **Food** with recent entries for this pet, **Amount put out (optional)**, **Unit**, **Save on this phone**. Keep the last choices for the next entry, but display them for confirmation; never submit a remembered amount invisibly. On save say **Saved on this phone · waiting for main to review**. Offline, double tap and retry still produce one operation. A pending event is separate from the accepted daily total. If the phone clock or timezone is suspect, ask the person to confirm When. The member cannot accept its own entry.

**Helper path:** A helper with explicit `pet_feeding.servings` permission may suggest a new event for Review with pet permanent ID, when and timezone, kind, food, optional bowl, amount and unit, source and reason. It cannot update or remove an existing event under the current add-only API. A duplicate-looking pet/time/food/amount should prompt **Possible repeat feeding entry** on Review with both sources and **Accept as another feeding** or **Reject this copy**. Do not silently deduplicate: two genuine servings can be close together. Acceptance revalidates that the pet still exists and that amount and unit are paired. A suggestion never affects Home or totals before acceptance.

**Daily view:** Heading **Food put out for Rex · 20/11/26** (date order follows household setting). Show accepted rows in time order, with bowl and food: **7:15 am · Kitchen bowl · Example dry dog food · 120 g**, **6:10 pm · Kitchen bowl · Example dry dog food · 120 g**. Summary **Recorded today: 240 g put out**. If units differ, show separate totals, e.g. **120 g and 1 pouch put out**; never add across units or infer pouch grams. If a row has no amount, show **Recorded today: 120 g and 1 pouch put out; 1 feeding has no amount** or **2 feedings recorded; amounts not measured**. This is a sum of saved entries, not proof of all meals or food eaten. Do not label it a complete daily intake. Show **1 change waiting for main** separately. A late accepted entry updates the historical day it belongs to, including when it was typed on a different day.

**Home card:** The Pets area can say **Rex · 2 feedings recorded today · 240 g put out** with a link **See feeding log**. If none, say **No feeding entry recorded today**, never **Rex has not been fed**. A stale phone copy says **From main's records as of [time]**. No feeding-related Needs attention warning or target is inferred: the household and vet decide what, how much and when to feed.

**Sheet:** A requested **Pet feeding log** print has a chosen pet and inclusive date range, one line per accepted event (local date/time, kind, bowl, food, amount and unit), then a daily subtotal **by unit** and a count of entries without amounts. It says **Food put out, not necessarily eaten. Check for entries made elsewhere.** A 14-day default is for manageable paper length only, not a feeding guideline. If the range is too long for a complete sheet, offer smaller ranges or a paged export with an explicit count; never silently stop after 200 rows. Private notes are omitted. No automatic full-year sheet.

**Scale:** About 700 events a year means around 1.9 per day. Index by pet and `given_at`, page the event list, and calculate a single pet/day total from accepted rows within that household-local day. A full-year overview can show monthly entry counts, with a clear missing-data meaning; it should not load 700 rows into one phone page. Keep all accepted events and corrections with stable IDs and revisions; test at least 1,000 rows, late entries, DST changes, multiple pets and mixed units. Do not use event absence as proof that a pet missed food.
