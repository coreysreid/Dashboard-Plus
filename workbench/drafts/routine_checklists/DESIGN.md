# Routine checklists as a view over actual work

Designed **21 November 2026** for batch 20 item 96. The proposed **Today**, **This week**, **This month** and **This year** views collect due work from existing modules plus household-created routines. They do not create a second completed flag for Home care or Garden. This is an engine design, not a shipped feature.

## One source of truth per task

| Source | What the list shows | What its tick actually does | Repeat rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| `home_care.jobs` with `next_due` | Job name, full Place path, Do again by date and, if set, the household's repeat | Opens the shipped **Mark done** form. One save adds `home_care.work_done`, sets Last done and asks for the next date; the routine view refreshes from that job. | Home care's repeat **offers** a date, never selects or advances it automatically (0091). No second routine recurrence. |
| `garden.jobs` with `due_on` and `done = false` | Unfinished Garden job, bed/Place and chosen date | Calls the shipped **Mark done** action, which sets `done = true`. A new Garden job is needed for another occurrence. | No repeat stored in Garden today; do not invent one from an old job. |
| `home_layout.equipment`, Medical kits/training, Pets treatment, Vehicles services and other existing due rows | Dated check or renewal with a link to its own record | **Open record to update**; do not offer a tick that merely hides its Needs attention reminder. | Their own date remains authoritative. A complete action can be added to that module later, with its own history. |
| Seasonal questions from `workbench/drafts/seasonal_checklists/` | Applicable review questions under the relevant season, with their authority and checked date | **Look at this question**; a person's answer/review date is saved by a future checklist-answer store, not as a Home care or Garden completion. | Applicability and review timing follow household hazard/region and source scope. A country alone cannot tick a question or pick a winter/heat season. |
| A new household routine | A plain task the person chose, its due occurrence and repeat choice | **Mark this occurrence done** writes one dated completion linked to that occurrence. | Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly only when explicitly chosen; no safety standard is inferred. |

Needs attention continues to show its existing due/overdue facts. The new view can link to those facts but must **not** add duplicate reminders or count one Home care job twice. Every row carries `(origin table, permanent ID, due date, occurrence ID if any)` and a stable route back to the source. A linked record changing or being deleted invalidates a cached row; the view recomputes and never retains a stale tick. A task with no date may be browsed under **No date yet**, not silently placed in Today.

## New routine and occurrence contract

A user-created `routine` stores name, optional full Place/People link, repeat count and unit (days/weeks/months/years), first due date, optional day/time, active/paused state, and which household calendar/timezone governs it. One `occurrence` stores scheduled date, state (`open`, `done`, `skipped`), completion or skip date, person and optional note, plus an operation ID/revision. A tick writes through the ordinary checked record path, with an idempotent operation ID: two taps create one completion. **Undo this tick** reverses only that completion while no later change depends on it. The next open occurrence is calculated **after** the current one is explicitly done or skipped. A repeat never performs the task and never marks a past date done.

For a late routine, show the oldest open due occurrence as **Due since [date]** in Today and the other date-range views where relevant. Do not create a stack of identical overdue cards. On **Mark done**, keep the actual done date and the scheduled date, then offer **Next planned date** from the household's repeat; a person can accept it, choose another, pause, or choose **No date yet**. If several repeat dates passed, record that gap as **Earlier times were not recorded** and schedule the next date the person chooses; never backfill completed ticks. **Skip this time** is a distinct, reversible decision, not a completion; ask for the next date. An item overdue by a month is still one calm action, not a streak, penalty or urgent badge.

For a monthly task on the 31st, offer the last day of a shorter month and keep the original anchor so March can return to the 31st; for a yearly 29 February, offer 28 February in a non-leap year, with a visible choice. Do not infer the repeat from the interval between old work dates. Changes to a routine repeat affect future occurrences only; preserve old completion history. At daylight-saving changes, local **due date** remains the household's chosen date, with a recorded zone for a timed occurrence. The household's week start follows its locale/setting, not a UTC boundary.

## Relationship to seasonal reviews

The five source-linked sets in `seasonal_checklists/CHECKLISTS.md` are **questions to review**, not a hidden set of automatic tasks. An answered seasonal question records who and when separately from the linked Stock, Home layout or People record. A positive water batch can be offered for inspection; it cannot auto-tick a question. A newly changed source record can invite another look. The official season dates in that draft are cues for selected cyclone/hurricane hazards; no fixed bushfire/winter/heat month is inferred. The person may move a review date. No general **Ready** score follows from ticks or answers.
