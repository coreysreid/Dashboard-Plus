# Four calm time windows

Designed **21 November 2026**. All example dates and jobs are invented.

Common heading **Things to do** with tabs **Today**, **This week**, **This month**, **This year**, plus **No date yet**. One row says the task, origin, full Place, the chosen due date and one primary action. A status word accompanies every color. Top summary: **3 things due or late in this view**; avoid a completion percentage or household readiness claim. Filters can hide a source but do not change it. At 320 px, the action follows the task words in reading/focus order. A screen reader hears the date and source before the button. All times and date boundaries use the household's locale and timezone.

**Today:** Shows due today **and** one open overdue occurrence per task, e.g. **Clean the gutters · Home care · Garage roof · Due since 18/11/26 · Mark done**, **Water seedlings · Garden · Backyard bed · Due today · Mark done**, **Check the smoke alarm · Our home · Check by 21/11/26 · Open record to update**. Home care's button opens its own date-choice form; Garden's sets done true; the alarm link does not hide an unedited reminder. A new routine row uses **Mark this occurrence done**. Empty: **Nothing is due or late from the dates you recorded. This does not mean every household task is done.**

**This week:** Shows tasks whose saved due date falls within the household's displayed week plus open overdue tasks, grouped **Late**, **Today**, **Later this week**. Show the exact week range, e.g. **Mon 16–Sun 22 Nov** for a Monday-start household. A task already done in this week appears under **Done this week** with date and **Undo** where safe; it is not another open card. If the household uses a different week start, both range and grouping change together. **Next week** is a preview, not a reason to mark work done early.

**This month:** Calendar-month range, not rolling 30 days. Group by week and source, with an optional **Seasonal questions to look at** section only for applicable household hazards. The review link says **Look at this question**, never **Mark done**. A job with no date appears under No date yet. Empty: **No recorded dates fall in this month. Add a date to a task you want to see here.**

**This year:** Calendar-year overview by month with counts of **due**, **done** and **skipped** from actual occurrences, then a list of next due rows. It does not materialize 365 daily cards or call an unrecorded day a missed task. Seasonal questions show their authority and check date. A routine due in the next calendar year stays out of this year; a late open occurrence from last year remains under **Still to decide** with its original date. Empty: **No dated work is recorded for this year.**

**After a tick:** **Marked done on 21/11/26. Next date: choose one** for a routine; Home care uses its shipped **When do you want to do it again?** form. If a repeat offers a date, label **Suggested from your repeat**, never **Automatically due**. **Skip this time** confirms **Skipped the 20/11/26 occurrence. No work was recorded as done.** **Undo this tick** restores only that occurrence if still safe. A stale open page says **This task changed while you were looking at it. Check the current date and try again. Nothing was saved.**
