# Important documents draft

A household can record where a passport, insurance policy, will or warranty is kept, and a date to review or renew it. This is a location and reminder list, not a digital document vault. Do not enter passport numbers, passwords, policy numbers or copies of private papers in the draft. A name and broad place are enough for many households. The date is optional and uses “due”, not “expired”, because it may be a review reminder rather than the document's legal expiry.

The current core cannot link this module to Home layout's draft places or print their full paths. Lane A should move the text `place` to a shared place link when Home layout is ready, with a household confirmation step for ambiguous names. Any future attachments would need separate privacy, backup and encryption decisions; this draft deliberately has none. Avoid a printed sheet listing sensitive storage locations until households can choose what to disclose.

This draft makes no claim about a country's renewal rules or legal validity. The household should follow each issuer's current advice and its own document.
