# Categories and tags a household can use

Batch 24 item 131, drafted **27 November 2026**. The [15 groups](groups.csv) and [120 leaf categories](categories.csv) are original starter words for a future Finance module. They are not a spending survey classification, tax advice, a live picker or a rule that decides what a bank line means. Every row has a stable key, an AU, US and UK display name, and optional search words separated by `|`, as in the Stock catalogue. A household can rename its own categories without changing those keys.

## What a household sees

On a transaction, choose **What was this for?**. The picker first shows **Uncategorised** and the household's recent categories, then a find box and the fifteen groups. Typing **rego**, **vehicle tax**, or **vehicle registration** finds the same `transport_registration_tax` key in each country, displayed in that household's words. Typing **rates**, **property tax**, or **council tax** finds `housing_property_tax`. The full group appears beside the result so **Pet food** cannot be mistaken for groceries. Changing country changes words, not saved keys or past transactions.

The starter tree covers the common spending areas in official AU, US and UK household expenditure classifications, then adds household-specific groups for pets, giving and money coming in. It deliberately does not copy an official detailed taxonomy. It has an **Other** leaf in several groups and **Uncategorised** when the person has not decided; neither means an amount is zero or omitted from the account balance. A household may add, rename or archive its own categories. A renamed starter key keeps its link to older transactions. Archiving a used category leaves history readable and asks the person before moving any past rows. An update to this CSV must not rename a household's own choice or silently recategorise its history.

Category is an explanation of a transaction; transaction **kind** and linked records determine accounting behavior. A transfer between the household's accounts has two linked legs and **no spending category**, even if a merchant or helper suggests one. Paying a card bill or the principal part of a mortgage is not new spending. `housing_mortgage_interest` is for a *known interest amount*, not a guess from a whole mortgage debit. A money-back event stays linked to the original expense, not a new income category. A split transaction's children may use several categories but their amounts must equal the one parent amount exactly; reports count the parent once and use children only to allocate it. A fee on a transfer is a separate expense if the person records one. The `flow` in `groups.csv` narrows the picker for ordinary income or expense; it never decides a bank CSV row's kind, sign, currency or whether it is a transfer.

## Free tags, distinct from categories

A tag is a household's own optional cross-cutting label, such as **Rex**, **Kitchen renovation**, or **Summer trip**. A pet's vet visit can have category **Pets › Vet care** and tag **Rex**; a hotel and a train ticket can have different categories and both use **Summer trip**. Tags do not add amounts or become a second category. The form offers existing tags and **Make a new tag**, but never creates one just because a bank description resembles it. A tag's own stable ID survives a rename; deleting a used tag asks whether to remove it from historical rows. Limit length and count in the core so a pasted statement cannot create thousands of tags. Free text goes through decision 0141's sensitive-detail guard. Tag names, custom category names and merchant patterns may reveal a person's habits, so they start local with Finance and follow decision 0140's explicit sharing choice before travelling to another computer.

## Merchant rules only suggest

After a person categorises an invented line **Example Market · 18.40**, offer **Suggest Groceries next time for Example Market?** with **Use this suggestion** or **No rule**. The saved rule contains a reviewed, exact normalised payee, a category key, optional account scope and a start date. It never stores the full raw bank line or account number. Normalisation may trim a bank's changing terminal reference only when the person sees the before/after match; a broad substring such as `market` is not a safe rule. A later imported line shows **Suggested Groceries by your Example Market rule** and offers **Keep**, **Choose another**, or **Leave uncategorised** on Review. It does not silently save a category, accept a bank import or create a transaction.

Manual choice takes precedence over any rule. An exact payee rule scoped to one account beats a household-wide exact rule. Two rules of equal scope that disagree leave the category undecided and explain the conflict. An unusual amount, refund, cash withdrawal, mixed basket, or a transaction whose payee changed substantially stays for a person's review even when the rule matches. A rule may be paused, edited, deleted and tested against invented previous lines without changing those lines. A supermarket can sell fuel, pharmacy items or gifts; the rule must not claim every line from one shop is always groceries.

A helper, if the household grants Finance read/suggestion access later, may propose an existing category key and a short reason for **Review**. It may not create a category or merchant rule, change a saved transaction, infer a transfer from a name, or bypass the person's choice. The response names the proposed key and group so similar words are clear. Existing helper permissions are separate from the household's computer-sharing permissions. Finance itself should start **Only on this computer**; a future choice to share records, tags or rules follows the current 0140 consent screen. No helper or module should request a bank login, password, full account/card number or ID copy (0141).

## Import and reporting traps

1. A bank CSV's sign or payee does not choose a category. A positive line might be a refund; a negative one might be a transfer. Preview the row and ask the person when meaning is unclear.
2. Repeated files, pending then posted lines, and two purchases at the same shop on one day must not collapse into one based on category. Category suggestions never change duplicate matching or balance arithmetic.
3. Regional labels are words, not tax treatment. **Council rates**, **property tax** and **council tax** are a search bridge, not a claim that the three laws or deductions are equivalent. The app must not label a category deductible or advise on a household's tax return.
4. Reports show uncategorised amounts and mixed currencies separately. They cannot call a month complete while relevant lines are waiting on Review. Category totals use reviewed transactions only, and tags are filters over those same transactions, not extra spending.

## Checks before promotion

1. Load both CSVs: exactly 15 unique group keys and 120 unique leaf keys, eight leaves per group, every `group_key` exists, all three regional names are present, one flow per group and no transfer leaf. Keep keys unchanged across a data update.
2. In AU, US and UK households search `rego`, `vehicle tax`, `council rates`, `HOA fees`, `service charges`, `petrol`, `gas`, `takeaway` and `takeout`. Show the regional display name and its group; country change never changes the key or a saved transaction.
3. Enter one income, expense, refund, paired transfer, card payment and mixed-category split in invented accounts. Check exact minor-unit balances and that a transfer and refund do not become extra spending from a category label or merchant rule.
4. Save two **Example Market** expenses on different accounts, one fuel purchase and one mixed receipt. An account-scoped rule suggests only for its account; no imported line or helper suggestion is accepted until the person chooses on Review. Conflicting rules stay undecided.
5. Rename and archive a used starter category and tag; history and a printed/exported report still identify old rows. A catalogue refresh does not reset the household's own name or mass-reclassify it.
6. Try a tag containing a full card number, a merchant description containing a full account number, a finance-only local tag and a helper's proposed category. The guard refuses fraud-grade text without echo, local values stay out of sync and helper output, and a private sharing change asks consent before a copy travels.
7. At 320 px, keyboard and screen reader, the picker names category **and group**, the source of a suggestion, the consequence of **Keep**, and the uncategorised option. Empty reports say what was recorded and what is waiting, not that spending was zero.

## Source basis

Checked **27 November 2026**: the [Australian Bureau of Statistics' A-COICOP household spending areas](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/detailed-methodology-information/concepts-sources-methods/australian-system-national-accounts-concepts-sources-and-methods/edition-8/chapter-10-gross-domestic-product-expenditure-approach-gdpe/household-final-consumption-expenditure), the [US Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Expenditure major categories](https://www.bls.gov/cex/ce-products.htm), and the [UK Office for National Statistics guide to COICOP categories](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/nationalaccounts/satelliteaccounts/methodologies/consumertrendsuk). Their broad areas helped check coverage; the 120 household leaf names and keys here are original. These official sources classify expenditure for statistics and do not validate a particular bank transaction, tax category or budget. The Stock [catalogue CSV](../catalogue/catalogue.csv) supplied the stable-key and `also_called` convention, not its product categories.
