# M2 restore and export acceptance checks

Use a made-up Smith household with two distinguishable snapshots (for example, 42 versus 47 Stock items), a second backup folder, a duplicate place name under two rooms, a private note, a deleted record and a text value beginning `=1+1`. Dates and counts are illustrative, not shipped data. Every case must leave the original data recoverable. Screen wording is in [FLOW.md](FLOW.md).

1. **Find by date.** With daily, close and before-update copies on different dates, the Backups page orders them newest first, filters by date and location, and shows full local time, kind and both physical locations for a duplicated copy. A failed second-copy status is shown separately from a good data-folder copy.
2. **Disconnected source.** Disconnect the made-up data-folder drive while a good copy remains on the computer. The page offers that copy, says the data folder is unavailable, and never labels the missing copy usable.
3. **Inspect before restore.** Select the older good copy. The preview shows timestamp, version, enabled modules, table counts and whether attachments are included; it distinguishes the 42-item snapshot from today's 47-item folder. No records are changed by preview.
4. **Reject damage.** Corrupt a copy or truncate it. Integrity validation marks it “Cannot restore this copy”; the UI gives a plain reason and does not offer the restore action. A second good copy remains selectable.
5. **New-folder restore.** Choose the older copy and an empty new destination. After validated copy, the compare page shows both folders and both counts. The old active folder remains byte-for-byte available; nothing switches until the person chooses. An existing nonempty destination is refused before writing.
6. **Failed copy is harmless.** Simulate a full destination disk or removal mid-copy. The current folder remains active and usable; no incomplete folder appears as a finished restored copy. The person sees “Restore did not finish. Your current records were not changed.”
7. **Switch and undo.** Switch to the restored folder. A verified snapshot of the former active folder exists. Edit one restored record, then choose Undo this restore. First snapshot the edited restored folder; switch back to the former folder with its 47 Stock items. The edited restored folder remains recoverable, and no merge is claimed.
8. **Two switches.** Restore twice in sequence. Undo switches in reverse order, identifying the source and destination each time, with no automatic deletion of any of the three folders.
9. **Export opens elsewhere.** Export every enabled table; open every CSV in both Excel and Google Sheets using their normal import flow. Names, Unicode, commas, line breaks, choice labels/keys, linked IDs/names and ISO dates remain legible. README identifies each file and column without needing Dashboard Plus.
10. **Private and dangerous cells.** A private medical note is present in the export and the pre-export warning names private data. Secrets and helper tokens are absent. Importing a cell beginning `=1+1`, `+`, `-` or `@` into Excel or Sheets must not execute a formula, while the original value remains recoverable from the documented lossless representation.
11. **Incomplete export.** Make one destination unwritable. The app reports an incomplete export rather than a ready one, lists the failed file, and leaves the active household data untouched.
12. **Missing optional data and attachments.** Export a household with no Pets and one with a made-up attachment. The first gets clear empty-table handling; the second includes the attachment with its link in README/CSV. A backup preview says truthfully whether that backup contains attachments.
