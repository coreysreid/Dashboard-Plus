# Drill acceptance checks

Written **21 November 2026**. Use only invented Smith, Garcia and Taylor records; none of the eight incidents is real.

1. Each scenario has stable ID/version, ordered original questions, source with checked date, applicability and a per-question exact record selector. The UI shows the record as an offer, not an automatic Yes.
2. Run the pipe drill with no water shut-off, then add one at a nested Place. The missing sentence changes to a full-path link; private access details remain behind Show and out of the drill answer/log.
3. Run bedroom and kitchen fire in a house and a purpose-built flat. Connections may show possible paths but never **two safe ways confirmed**. The flat asks for its building plan instead of universally prescribing leave or stay put; the outside meeting place is a person's own answer.
4. For a break-in, general list, Home, notifications, helper read and export contain no room-by-room hiding plan, access code or private answer. The drill names only the checked local emergency number; a country without one checked gets **Use your local emergency number**.
5. A car without roadside contact or vehicle kit shows the exact missing words. A kit last recorded in a vehicle is not assumed present now. UK motorway source steps never appear as AU/US road advice.
6. A 72-hour power drill with no recorded power-dependent needs or light supplies says so without claiming no one depends on electricity. No food is called safe for 72 hours, and an indoor generator is never suggested.
7. For a hypothetical regional water warning, only positive litres in water items answered safe to drink count toward the displayed estimate. The official live advisory, area, affected uses and update time must be person checked; no blanket boil instruction appears.
8. A cyclone/hurricane forecast fits only the relevant region and selected hazard. It offers BOM for AU or NWS for US with a local warning check; a track near a suburb does not become an evacuation instruction.
9. Save a partially answered run: **Continue later** does not set Practised on. Complete it: own answers, selected record IDs/revisions, practice date and chosen review date persist, while old runs remain in history. A changed/deleted/moved record asks for review without overwriting the answer.
10. A person who answers an existing Readiness prompt during a drill sees the same answer on Questions; a narrative drill answer is not forced into an ordinary `readiness__answers.note` that travels. Helpers cannot answer.
11. Print/accessibility at 320 px, large text, keyboard and screen reader: each source, question, record offer, missing state, answer and review date is readable in order. No screen claims a practice run makes the household safe or ready.
12. Break every source URL in a test fixture or make it older than its checked date. The drill retains the citation and marks it **Source needs checking**; it does not silently substitute unsourced advice. No live warning is presented from a stale cached page.
