# Safety sources and limits

Opened and checked **21 November 2026**. The eight situations and all question wording are original to Dashboard Plus. [INFLUENCES.md](../../../docs/INFLUENCES.md) credits only the general idea of thinking realistic situations through ahead of time; no book's selection, order, case or words were used. Links are to public safety bodies unless marked as a utility provider. Country-specific advice is labelled, and the household's local warning or building plan takes priority. This page is a source ledger, not live emergency advice.

| Key | Source and checked scope | Fact used in the drills |
|---|---|---|
| P | [American Red Cross, Make a Plan](https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/make-a-plan.html), US general planning | A household can plan communication, meeting and practice ahead of a crisis. It does not give a pipe-specific procedure. |
| L | [Water Corporation WA, Leaks](https://www.watercorporation.com.au/Help-and-advice/Water-issues/Leaks), Australian public utility | Its Leaks page existed, but retrieved text did not establish a specific shut-off instruction. The pipe drill therefore asks where the household's recorded water shut-off is and what it would check; it does not tell people to operate equipment. |
| F | [US Fire Administration, Home Fire Escape Plans](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/home-fire-escape-plans/) and [Fire and Rescue NSW, Home escape plans](https://www.fire.nsw.gov.au/page.php?id=883), US/AU | Plan and practise ways out, consider people needing help, identify an outside meeting place, and stay out once out. NSW says draw two ways from each room; a Dashboard room graph cannot certify them. FRNSW names 000 for an emergency in NSW. |
| K | [US Fire Administration, Cooking Fire Safety](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prevent-fires/cooking/), US | Unattended cooking is a fire risk; its public advice says stay with cooking and turn a burner off if leaving the kitchen. The drill asks about people, exits and alarm, without teaching a firefighting technique. |
| B | [UK government, 999 and 112 national emergency numbers](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/999-and-112-the-uks-national-emergency-numbers), UK, and [US National 911 Program](https://www.911.gov/), US; FRNSW above for AU 000 | Use the local emergency number first for immediate danger: 000 AU, 911 US, 999 UK. No tactical break-in instruction is taken from these pages. Do not show those numbers for another country without its own official source. |
| R | [National Highways, Driving on motorways: what to do if you break down](https://nationalhighways.co.uk/road-safety/breakdowns/), UK motorway scope | Breakdown location and road type matter; live-lane/motorway procedure is specific. The drill asks where a vehicle is, how to reach help, and which local road authority advice applies; it does not give UK motorway steps to AU/US/NZ households. |
| E | [US CDC, What to Do to Protect Yourself During a Power Outage](https://www.cdc.gov/natural-disasters/response/what-to-do-protect-yourself-during-a-power-outage.html), US | Power cuts affect food, medical needs and carbon monoxide risk from indoor generator use. The drill asks which household needs depend on power and where to check official updates; it does not promise stored food is safe for 72 hours. |
| W | [US CDC, How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency](https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/index.html), US | Emergency tap water may be unsafe; water with fuel or toxic chemicals cannot be made safe by ordinary boiling/disinfection. The drill therefore asks for the *actual advisory's* instructions and excludes tap water from a hypothetical safe supply until the authority clears it. No universal boil instruction is given. |
| C | [Australian Bureau of Meteorology, Tropical cyclone](https://www.bom.gov.au/cyclone/), AU | It provides cyclone warnings and forecast-track information. The drill asks which official warnings cover the household's location and when it will check again, not whether a forecast line proves an impact. The page's live content was unavailable in the retrieved snapshot; no current storm claim is made. For the US, [National Weather Service, What to Do Before the Tropical Storm or Hurricane](https://www.weather.gov/safety/hurricane-plan) was also opened: it points households to local evacuation-area information, a family plan and NWS watches/warnings. No current storm claim is made. |

For a shared flat, [London Fire Brigade's flat escape guidance](https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/the-home/escape-plan/escape-plan-blocks-of-flats/) was also checked **21 November 2026**: building arrangements may call for staying put or evacuating. The fire drills ask for the household's actual building instruction rather than infer a universal route. None of these sources is a substitute for a local authority's live warning during an incident.
