# Household money, without a bank login

Batch 24 item 130. Planned **27 November 2026**. **Finance does not exist in the app yet.** Bills and insurance records dates to check, not whether a bill was paid. Stock's **What you paid** covers only priced Stock ins, and **Money back** covers a refund tied to one such Stock in; its **Spent per month** page already says it is incomplete. A Finance total must use its own confirmed transactions and must not silently add those other partial amounts or make duplicate payments.

## A household's first path

1. Switch on **Money** only if wanted. It opens with **Nothing recorded here yet. This is for tracking your own accounts and transactions; it does not connect to a bank.** Add an account named in household words: **Everyday account**, **Savings**, **Cash in wallet**, or **Card we pay off**. Choose its type and currency. **Last 4 digits (optional)** takes only four digits; never ask for a full account or card number, online banking login, PIN, security answer or statement image. An opening amount is a dated, person-confirmed **Starting balance**, not a purchase or income.
2. **Add a transaction** asks account, day, description, money in or money out, amount and optional category/note. Show the amount with that account's currency before Save. The account page lists entries in date order with the balance worked out from confirmed entries; it never says that the bank agrees until the household has checked a statement. Use **Show amounts** before revealing account balances, entries or charts on a shared screen. A cash account is a manually counted account; no inferred bank balance.
3. **Move money between my accounts** asks From, To, day and amount, then previews **100.00 AUD leaves Everyday account and arrives in Savings. This is one move, not spending.** Save both linked sides or neither. If a card account is shown with a negative balance, explain the amount owed in words. Different currencies need a separately entered sent amount and received amount, and belong to a later release; never invent an exchange rate.
4. **Bring in transactions from a file** starts with a file the person downloaded themselves, never a bank login or automatic connection. The first version accepts a selected bank CSV profile only. The app reads it on this computer, asks which existing account it belongs to, maps date, description, money in/out and amount, and previews **Ready**, **Choose a date/account**, **Possible repeat**, or **Cannot bring in** per row. Nothing enters the ledger until the person accepts reviewed rows. The raw file is not kept. A second import of the same rows offers **Already brought in** with the earlier match, while two real purchases with the same day and amount are not silently collapsed.
5. **This month** shows confirmed money in, money out and transfers separately, one currency at a time, with a table beneath the chart. **Unsorted** and **not checked against a statement** stay visible. Say **From the transactions you recorded or brought in** rather than **Everything your household spent**. The first version can show a monthly category table once item 131's starter categories exist; no automatic judgement about a merchant.

At 320 px, the account name, amount and status remain in reading order without sideways scrolling. Empty periods read **No transactions recorded for this month**, not **No spending**. An account that was not imported for a period has an explicit coverage gap. Corrections have history and Undo, with balances and reports recalculated from the corrected entries.

## What belongs in the first module and what comes later

| Stage | Scope a household can trust | Why it waits or fits now |
|---|---|---|
| First module | Named current/checking, savings, card and cash accounts; optional last four; one currency each; dated starting balance; manual money-in/out transactions; same-currency paired transfers; read-only account balance and monthly table; reviewed CSV import for a small number of tested bank layouts; correction and Undo. | Establish one reliable ledger and its privacy, repeat and balance rules before charts, automation or budget claims. No bank credentials, external service or raw statement storage. |
| Next | Split a transaction across categories; category and free-tag choices from item 131; monthly budgets; recurring Bill links; explicit refund links; category bars and printable summaries. | Needs a stable transaction identity and category coverage. Splits and Bill matching must not turn one payment into two. A budget needs chosen period, currency, whether refunds offset it and a clear treatment of uncategorised rows. |
| Later | More bank CSV layouts, local OFX and QIF file parsers, credit-card reconciliation, cross-currency transfers, multi-year comparison, household-defined merchant suggestions and controlled helper suggestions for Review. | File formats and bank exports vary; unsupported versions must be refused intact. Cross-currency totals need explicit human-entered values, never a guessed rate. Merchant rules and helper suggestions need a preview and a person deciding. |

An OFX or QIF file is a **local file option**, not permission to ask for an online bank username or connect to an OFX server. OFX has older 1.x SGML and newer 2.x XML forms, so one parser claim does not cover every export. QIF is plain text and can carry account and transaction details. CSV column orders and signs vary by provider. Support should be named by tested export profile and version, with an **Unsupported file; nothing was added** outcome, not a partial guess.

## Money rules that keep the totals honest

- Keep amounts exactly to the smallest unit of their own currency; reject a row whose amount or currency is ambiguous. Do not add AUD, USD and GBP, convert them automatically or count an empty amount as zero.
- One transfer has two linked account entries in one save: equal and opposite amounts in the same currency. It changes each account's balance, but contributes **zero** to income and spending totals. An imported debit and credit that look like a transfer are only **Possible move between accounts** until the person links them; never auto-pair by amount alone.
- A split has one parent transaction and category allocations whose exact amounts sum to it. The account balance counts the parent once; category totals count its children, never the parent plus children. A correction or Undo changes them together.
- A refund is a separate dated money-in transaction, linked to the original payment only if the person confirms it. It can be reported beside gross spending; a refund in December does not silently shrink November's bar. A Stock **Money back** record does not automatically create a Finance entry, and a Finance refund does not change Stock quantity. Reconciliation between them is an explicit later action.
- A Bill date is a reminder, never proof of a payment or active policy. A future Bill-to-transaction link may say **Possible payment for this bill — check it**. Its chosen one-to-one match never creates a second transaction or a second reminder. Repeating a bill does not generate a payment until one is recorded or imported.
- A budget is a household-chosen monthly amount for a category or group in one currency. No rollover, tax treatment or future forecast is assumed. Show **3 of 8 transactions still unsorted** and say that incomplete imports make comparisons incomplete; do not declare success or overspending from a partial month.

## Bringing in bank files, without keeping the dangerous bits

Use the invented [example export](example-bank-export.csv) only to rehearse mapping; it is not a promise that a real bank uses those columns. The person chooses the account and date order, checks debit/credit signs and previews every row in their usual date display. A row with a blank or ambiguous date, unknown account, mixed currency, impossible amount, or a possible repeat waits for a decision. A file-level problem rejects the whole file before any save. Accepting chosen rows is one atomic operation with one Undo; a retry after interruption cannot double them.

The import's first action is to inspect the file safely in memory, not save it under `data/`, logs, browser history, Review or a backup. Bank exports can contain full account or card numbers, routing details, names, addresses and even credentials in headers or comments. Decision 0141's existing card-number check is necessary but not enough for every bank-file spelling. Before any browser preview or persistent staging, detect and remove forbidden values, show **This file includes details this app does not keep. Remove them from your copy and try again** without echoing them, or skip a clearly bounded metadata field under a reviewed rule. Never persist the original bytes, filenames with private text, or a parser exception containing a source line. The ordinary field guards still run on every accepted value. Limit file size, row count and XML/SGML features; prohibit entity expansion, external references and network fetches in an OFX parser.

For repeat matching, prefer a stable export transaction ID **scoped to the selected account**, when the tested format has one; keep only the minimum local matching token. Also show date, amount, direction and description as a candidate comparison. A repeated file/row with a changed description is shown for review, not silently copied or rejected. Pending and posted versions may be one event, but need an explicit confirmed match; two same-price visits on the same day may be two real events. Never use a bank's displayed running balance as if it were an imported transaction.

## Privacy and sharing

Propose Finance lists as **Only on this computer** by default, with every transaction amount, description, category, account name, last four and notes private behind Show. A household can choose what reaches its other computers under decision 0140, with an explicit confirmation before private history travels; a first pairing snapshot must follow the effective choices. A member's report must say when it has only part of the accepted ledger. Helpers have separate access choices, and the first Finance module offers them no transaction writes or private reads. A future helper may suggest a category for Review (item 131), never fetch a bank file, log in, confirm a price or accept its own suggestion.

Decision 0141 still applies when the household has chosen to share: no full account or card number, bank login, security answer, ID scan or copy is stored at all. Last four is for recognition only, not verification. The data folder and its backups still hold private financial history; **Show** is a screen privacy control, not encryption or a promise that a lost computer is safe. Printed/exported finance data needs a separate explicit choice and a complete/incomplete label before it is designed.

## Invented acceptance walk-through

1. Add **Everyday account**, **Savings** and **Cash in wallet** in AUD, each with optional last four only. A card account starts at 0; a 30.00 purchase makes its statement-style balance −30.00 and reads **30.00 owed**. A full account/card number, PIN or password in any name, note or import header is refused without echo or storage.
2. Record 100.00 AUD out of Everyday and into Savings as one transfer. Both balances change; monthly spending remains unchanged. A second press, crash before commit and Undo leave either both sides or neither. A cross-currency attempt asks for a later supported flow, not a made-up rate.
3. Record 24.00 AUD at **Example Grocer**, split 18.00 Groceries and 6.00 Pet supplies. The account changes by 24.00 once; both category rows sum to 24.00. Correct the total to 25.00: the old split cannot remain silently wrong. Save a 5.00 refund on a later day and compare gross and money back in words.
4. Bring in the invented CSV; map its headings and account, preview date/order/sign. Re-import it and an overlapping second file. Rows with an exact scoped ID say **Already brought in**; same-day same-amount distinct rows remain possible repeats for the person to choose. A pending/posted pair, reversed debit sign and ambiguous `05/06/27` each ask instead of guessing. A rejected file adds zero rows.
5. Link a confirmed payment to one Bills reminder. Neither the reminder nor the transaction is duplicated; the reminder never claims a policy is active. A priced Stock in and Stock Money back do not enter the Finance total unless the person records or matches their bank events explicitly.
6. Switch Finance off, restore a backup, then choose to share only its non-amount details. Verify no amount, bank-file metadata or last four leaks through lists, search, Needs attention, addresses, charts, print, export, helpers, Review or the sync snapshot. Start sharing amounts with confirmation, then tighten the choice; no screen promises to recall copies already on members.
7. At 320 px, desktop, print preview, keyboard and a screen reader, check that amount visibility, table totals, missing-month words, currency, transfer exclusion, unsorted count and source coverage remain understandable. A month with no imported records must not say the household spent nothing.
