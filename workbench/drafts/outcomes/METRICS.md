# What your records did for you

**Draft checked 2026-11-17.** Use the household's local calendar months and timezone. A “full month” means every calendar day in it was inside the period during which the household was recording the relevant facts. Compare the **last completed month** with the one before it; show the current partial month separately, without a change claim. Recompute from saved records and edits, not from a cached card. Give each card a **How worked out** link listing its included records, excluded records and coverage dates. Never describe a difference as a saving or improvement without the household confirming an outcome.

The four measures below separate **what was recorded** from **what happened in real life**. Missing records mean “We don't have enough history,” not zero. A household may have thrown something away without logging it or bought something elsewhere. The UI must never infer the opposite.

## Comparison rules

1. Use the household timezone's calendar boundaries, not UTC midnight or a rolling 30-day window. In the invented example, October has 31 days and September 30. If the timezone changes, preserve the originally recorded local date and flag any period that must be rebuilt.
2. A zero requires an explicit **complete-through** statement for that measure and period. A bare absence of movements, receipts, or answers is unknown. Preserve the date and person who confirmed completeness, and offer **“This month is incomplete”** if they retract it.
3. Deletions, corrections, imports, late entries and returned items may revise a prior month. Show **“Updated after a later entry”** and the current evidence; never silently keep an old “win.” Deduplicate imported receipt lines by stable source IDs before summing.
4. For Stock amounts, use the unit saved at the time of the movement. A later change from `packs` to `each` must not reinterpret old amounts. If an item was deleted, keep an evidence label without exposing private notes.
5. No percentage change when the earlier month is zero or has incomplete history. Use counts and the period basis. Even when a measure moves in a favourable direction, say **“recorded entries fell”**, not **“you reduced waste.”**
6. Readiness applicability can change when the home, household or hazards change. Recompute the current denominator, but compare prior months only over the *same applicable prompt IDs* and version; otherwise say **“The questions changed, so there is no direct comparison.”** A `not_relevant` answer is a reviewed question, not a Yes.
7. Medical kit checks and training renewals can be linked as supporting records to a *person-confirmed* readiness win. A completed check or a sheet printed alone does not establish safety.

## Confirmed wins

A win is a separate `core__outcomes` proposal, as in `docs/DESIGN.md` section 10.4: kind, title, amount/unit if known, linked evidence and `proposed`, `confirmed` or `dismissed` state. The household must choose **“Yes, this helped”** and may edit the words. Example: **“You recorded three fewer discarded entries this month. Did anything you changed help?”** A Yes can make a household-written note; it does not prove the cause. For money, ask for the household's own usual cost and actual replacement, then show the arithmetic and currency before asking **“Did this really replace that expense?”** Never derive a money saving from the difference between monthly shopping totals.
