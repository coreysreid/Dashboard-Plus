# The next three months, in household words

Batch 24 item 137. Proposed **28 November 2026** for Lane A and User 1 to choose from; these are **priorities and decision gates, not promised ship dates**. One major new trust boundary at a time is more useful than ten partly working features. At each four-week review, keep the next step only if its acceptance checks pass and User 1 still wants it next.

## Starting point

Windows has a portable USB folder, Mac has an **unsigned Apple silicon preview**, and neither an installed Windows release nor a signed Mac release is ready for households. Permanent record IDs, revisions, a filtered change log and **What your other computers get** exist, but computers cannot pair and no phone app exists. The current calendars, bars, dots and timelines already show useful records; a household-built view, comparison chart and its own alert rules do not. Bills tracks dates to check, Stock has some confirmed prices and money back, and there is no Finance ledger or built-in photo Inbox. These are the boundaries behind the order below, checked against current main on **28 November 2026**.

The third-month list is a **ranked queue**. A trusted money ledger plus one useful saved view may use all of the capacity. The remaining plans do not become rushed features merely because they appear here. Splits, budgets, recurring Bill/payment matching, OFX/QIF parsing, automatic Mac/Windows updates and broad Finance reports follow the first ledger only after its balance, import and recovery checks pass. A photo Inbox remains **off** until local decoding and forbidden-detail screening prove that no image or unsafe OCR text reaches disk, logs, backup, Review or a helper. A text/manual Inbox can be tried sooner without making that promise.

## What remains deliberately later

- A phone App Store or Play release, background sync, home server, public network endpoint and automatic member acceptance. A phone can only follow the reviewed two-computer protocol and the household's sharing choices.
- A Finance connection that logs in to a bank; full account/card numbers and ID copies are outside the product by decision 0141. Budgets, splits, foreign-currency conversion and merchant automation need person-confirmed ledger rules first.
- A chart that claims food **eaten** from food **put out**, a causal conclusion from correlation, or a health warning from a household-chosen percentage. The [Rex fixture](../correlations/PLAN.md) needs week-completeness facts before it becomes a live comparison.
- Inbox photo storage or an online OCR shortcut. The safe first step is structured text to Review; an external helper's own photo service requires separate consent and cannot be described as staying on this computer.

## How to review this order

At the end of each four-week block, use an invented household and User 1's one-hour script to answer: **Can the person finish the new job, recover from a mistake, explain what is on another device, and tell what the chart or total leaves out?** Record a failed gate and choose the next local task; do not silently weaken the gate or publish a partial release. Lane B checks the shipped experience after each merge and reports exact words and priority order.
