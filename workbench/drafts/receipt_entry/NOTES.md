# Evidence and core needs for receipt entry

**Batch 19 item 83, design only (21 November 2026):** [UNMATCHED_LINES.md](UNMATCHED_LINES.md) specifies a distinct unmatched-product-line entry beside ordinary source-bearing suggestions, plus exact Choose item, Skip line, Undo skip and Finish receipt review actions. Core must add a separate Review row kind and state, validation for hints, a group that stays open while a line needs a choice, a stale-safe person-confirmed Stock in transaction, and a way to keep source text in Review history without copying it into Stock. The connector schema and API contract must extend together. Current Review cannot stage or decide such a row. Receipt-wide Undo and duplicate detection remain separate gaps.

Checked 17 November 2026. Retailer pages describe **features to allow for**, not a universal receipt grammar. The example lines in FLOW.md and `match_table.csv` are entirely invented and carry no retailer text, prices or customer information. The 200 candidates are selected for plausible household shopping coverage from the catalogue, not verified by a national frequency dataset. Lane A should test actual receipts with the owner's consent and avoid treating this vocabulary as a guarantee.

**Helper update checked 19 November 2026:** [HELPER_FLOW.md](HELPER_FLOW.md) describes what the shipped MCP connector and helper API can do now. The assistant reads receipt text or a photo in **its own app**, looks up granted Stock items, and stages new Stock movements for the person to Accept/Reject on Dashboard's Review. If the assistant uses an online service, the receipt and records it reads may reach that service; cover payment, loyalty and address details first. Dashboard does not currently parse a photo or offer a receipt upload to helpers. The earlier [FLOW.md](FLOW.md) remains a future built-in screen design; its “no receipt is sent away” applies only to a future local parser, not an online assistant.

**After decision 0097, checked 21 November 2026:** The shipped helper path now accepts a per-entry `source` line and price for Review, and `{"ref": N}` links a new item to its first Stock in within one suggestion request. Review groups the linked rows, accepts dependencies together, and rejects dependent rows when their new item is rejected. The source is retained on the proposal for Review, not copied into Stock records or general exports. A live connector and two-width browser rerun with the existing invented receipt plus extra invented test lines is in [the batch 19 report](../../reports/2026-11-21-receipt-group-review.md). The old two-line [CONNECTOR_RUN.md](CONNECTOR_RUN.md) remains a dated account of the earlier behavior, not the current feature list.

**Remaining helper limits:** The helper still needs to read a photo or text in its own app; Dashboard has no receipt image tool. An unmatched line cannot yet be held on Review with Choose item or Skip line, and one helper request has no receipt-wide Undo. Review cannot edit a proposed item, amount, date or place before Accept. A source price is for checking and is not a Stock price. Duplicate receipt detection is not shipped; `request_key` only makes one helper request safe to retry. A local parser, ambiguity choices, duplicate checks and careful undo still need their own core work. Do not infer use-by dates, storage places or drinking-water flags from a catalogue hint.

**Item 67 live run, checked 20 November 2026:** [CONNECTOR_RUN.md](CONNECTOR_RUN.md) records a synthetic receipt image read by the assistant, exact MCP calls, the connector's waiting reply, two browser Review accepts and before/after Smith Stock amounts and batch counts. The connector has no image tool: a named assistant app would need to read the photo itself before calling these tools. The current Review lacks original receipt lines and price context. The run deliberately left `expires` and `place` out, so normal Stock logic made two new undated/unplaced batches; a receipt-aware flow needs a person-supplied place and actual date where relevant, plus provenance and an atomic receipt Undo. Do not invent these from the catalogue or save a raw receipt with payment details by default.

## What Lane A needs from the core

The current Stock forms accept one movement at a time. For this flow the core needs: a local-only receipt text parser and optional local image/OCR path; extraction of product lines separately from totals, tax and promotions; quantity/unit parsing with a distinction between purchased quantity, package size and weighed amount; catalogue candidate ranking using aliases, region and existing household items; a staged review record that saves nothing until confirmed; a batch write/undo transaction with conflict detection; and duplicate-receipt detection without retaining payment details. Store only the approved line text needed for an audit trail, with a household option to discard it. The app must not contact a remote OCR service without a separate opt-in and privacy design.

Do not add inferred use-by dates, locations or drinking-water flags just because text matched a catalogue entry. Confirm those through the normal Stock/lots rules. For food bought by weight, compare compatible units before proposing a movement. If the parser cannot separate `2 for $7` from `2L`, show the whole line for a person to fix instead of guessing.
