# Readiness screen acceptance checks

Use the 49 draft prompt IDs and synthetic household settings and records. The current core has no prompt engine; these are checks for Lane A to implement. Exact screen text is in [SCREENS.md](SCREENS.md).

1. **Calm budget.** Home shows at most two questions, each with its `question` and `why`. “Not now” defers without increasing coverage, setting a Not relevant answer or making a safety claim.
2. **Four actions.** Open `fire-upper-floor`; Yes, Not relevant, Not yet and Tell me more are available. Tell me more expands reason, official source link and checked date without changing the answer or coverage. Each of the other three saves a dated person-selected answer.
3. **Applicability.** `fire-upper-floor` fits a two-storey Smith home but not a one-storey Taylor home. A prompt with `fits = {hazards = ["flood"]}` is filtered only from a household's stated hazards. The Taylor exclusion is “does not fit settings”, not a fabricated person-selected Not relevant answer.
4. **Count.** For 49 applicable prompts, 20 Yes, 2 Not relevant, 1 Not yet and 26 unanswered show “23 of 49 questions looked at.” Tell me more on an unanswered one leaves 23. If only 35 apply, the denominator is 35 and the page explains the other 14. Never label this percentage “safe”, “prepared” or a pass.
5. **No invented upstairs match.** Smith has an upstairs route in Home layout's Ways out, but it does not say whether everyone can leave when stairs are blocked. `fire-upper-floor` offers no automatic record match; the person still sees all four actions and may open Ways out to write a plan. A future purpose-built field must be reviewed before adding a match.
6. **Exact matching.** For each of the eight non-`none` `answered_by` fields, apply its row-specific conditions in NOTES.md. All 41 `none` prompts offer no automatic record match. A power-category item must not satisfy the torch question; a single pet row must not answer a question about all pets. For `heat-heat-water`, use a positive batch at `stock.lots.place` with a drinkable-litre Water item, not the old item-level place.
7. **Private candidate.** A People medication detail may offer a private “record may relate” notice but its text is absent from Home and ordinary printed views. A blank or wrong-kind detail cannot match. A person still chooses an answer.
8. **Water candidate.** A positive Water item in litres, Safe to drink, with a linked place may be offered for `heat-heat-water`; Flushing water, distilled appliance water, a zero quantity or a non-litre unit is not offered. None is auto-Yes.
9. **Not yet and review.** Not yet saves an answer date and reminder date; it counts as looked at now. At the chosen date the question appears in Review again, with its prior answer still in history and current coverage reduced until a new answer.
10. **Linked record changed.** After Yes linked to a Medical kit place, move or delete the place or kit. The answer is flagged for review; the historical answer remains, and the reminder reveals no private data. A new Yes requires a person to confirm again.
11. **Changed household settings.** Turn Pets on or add Flood as a stated hazard. Recompute applicability and denominator without rewriting previous answers. An answer to a now-inapplicable question remains in history; it is outside the current applicable count until settings change back.
12. **Sources and dates.** Every shown prompt links its own official source with the stored checked date; the household answer date is distinct. A source due for editorial review does not become a household due date or a safety warning.
