# Try Dashboard Plus for one hour

This is a solo run on your own Windows 10 or 11 computer. **Use made-up names and goods**, in a fresh extracted folder set aside for this trial. Do not open your real household's `data` folder, and do not share any real phone number, address, medical detail or helper key in the feedback. Set a 60-minute timer. If a step stalls for more than two minutes, note where you got stuck and move on. You are testing how the product feels, not whether you can make every screen work.

Have a pen or a copy of [FEEDBACK.md](FEEDBACK.md) beside you. After each task, jot down anything surprising in the **Your notes** space. The times below add to **60 minutes**; they are guides, not deadlines.
