# Try Dashboard Plus for one hour

This is a solo run on your own Windows 10 or 11 computer. **Use made-up names and goods**, in a fresh extracted folder set aside for this trial. Do not open your real household's `data` folder, and do not share any real phone number, address or medical detail in the feedback. Set a 60-minute timer. If a step stalls for more than two minutes, note where you got stuck and move on. You are testing how the product feels, not whether you can make every screen work.

Have a pen or a copy of [FEEDBACK.md](FEEDBACK.md) beside you. After each task, jot down anything surprising in the **Your notes** space. The times below add to **60 minutes**; they are guides, not deadlines.

| # | Minutes | Do this | Notice |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | From the [project's Actions page](https://github.com/coreysreid/Dashboard-Plus/actions), open the newest green `main` run, download **dashboard-plus-windows** from Artifacts, and extract its inner zip. Use a fresh **Dashboard Plus** folder. | Could you tell which download and folder to use? Did Windows block or warn? |
| 2 | 6 | Double-click **Open Dashboard**. On Welcome, enter two invented household members, choose your country and region, a home type, and switch on **People, Household stock, Pets and Medical readiness**. Finish setup. | Did it open without help? Were the questions clear and was the starting page useful? |
| 3 | 4 | In **People**, add an invented contact called **Alex Example**, phone **0491 570 156** if you choose Australia, **(415) 555-0156** in the US, or **07700 900156** in the UK. In **Who to call**, make Alex an Emergency contact. For another country, use no phone number. | Could you see where contacts and their roles differ? Could you find Alex again? |
| 4 | 5 | In **Places**, add **Kitchen**, **Pantry** inside Kitchen, **Shelf 3** inside Pantry, and **Garage**. Open Shelf 3 and find its full path. | Did “inside” make sense? Was **Kitchen › Pantry › Shelf 3** visible in lists and pickers? |
| 5 | 20 | Add the twenty demo Stock items in the table below. Start typing each name and pick a suggestion when it appears; use your own typed name if it does not. Use the shown amount, unit and **Keep at least** value. Put pantry goods on Shelf 3, tools in Garage, water in Kitchen and first aid in Kitchen. Type the shown **Use by** dates in your country's order; blank means leave it empty. For **Bottled drinking water**, answer **Yes, count these litres as drinking water**. | Did the suggestions save typing? Was adding the next item quick? Did the place picker show the full path? Did a date show in words before Save? Count how many items you finished in 20 minutes. |
| 6 | 4 | In **Stock in and out**, choose Kidney beans, **Bought or added**, 2 tins, today's date. Return to Kidney beans and check its amount. | Could you find the item, and did the count change as you expected? |
| 7 | 3 | Open **Needs attention**. Find the low items, the Use by dates and days of drinking water. | Can you tell how the water estimate was worked out? Is anything urgent or confusing? |
| 8 | 4 | In **First aid kits**, add **Home first aid kit**, choose **Usually kept: At home** and **Where it is: Kitchen**. Set **Last checked** to today's date and **Check again by** to a date you choose. | Could you tell the difference between “At home” and the exact place? Did the kit-at-home question respond? |
| 9 | 4 | In **Pets**, add a made-up dog called **Pip**, then open Pip's record and add a pretend vet contact if time allows. | Was a pet easy to add and find? Were any medical details asked unnecessarily? |
| 10 | 6 | From Home, open **Print: Emergency sheet (fridge copy)**. Check whether Alex appears and whether anything private appears unexpectedly. You can preview without printing paper. Return to the app, choose **Save and exit**, then **Save and exit now**; wait for **Saved and closed**. | Was the sheet useful as it stood? Could you tell when it was safe to close or unplug? |
