# Medical readiness draft notes

This files-only draft records first aid kits, dated kit checks and first aid or CPR training renewals. Kit locations link to Stock places; checkers and trainees link to People. Kit contents stay in Stock's first-aid category. It does not give medical advice.

Open questions for Lane A: should a kit check list specific Stock items, or is a note enough? Should a completed check generate the next check date, or should the person enter it? Which training records may safely appear on a printed sheet?

Core features needed: a due-soon view for kit checks, and prompts such as “Is there a first aid kit?” The current watch view handles training renewal dates but not per-kit check schedules. Official guidance links and a review process are needed before prompts ship.
