# Core work, privacy and source reuse

Checked **21 November 2026**: decision 0103, shipped `stock.places` and `stock.connections`, [Fire and Rescue NSW](https://www.fire.nsw.gov.au/page.php?id=883), and the two [London Fire Brigade escape-plan pages](https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/the-home/escape-plan/). The detailed source-to-claim table is in [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). This draft uses original wording, no copied diagrams or images. The research supports prompts for planning and practice; it does **not** validate any household's route.

Core currently has room nesting and one undirected connection per pair, but no floor/room classification, outside-destination status, possible-window marker, meeting place, help-needs acknowledgement, shared-building-plan acknowledgement, graph drawing, route independence checker or complete map print. A connection may be a door or stairs but is not a verified passable exit. Build explicit household confirmation and a dated review state. If the product cannot identify independent routes, show only recorded possibilities and never a **two ways out confirmed** badge. Shared-building instructions need their own prompt and links to local authority guidance; do not derive “stay put” or evacuation from the map.

Decision 0103 makes room names, connections and notes ordinary fields that travel and can be read by a helper with Stock access. Do not add codes or key locations to this draft. A print preview needs a layout-disclosure warning, and the text alternative must exclude Home layout's private `access_details`. Check the actual region's fire authority and building manager before household use; NSW and London pages are examples of guidance checked, not universal rules.
