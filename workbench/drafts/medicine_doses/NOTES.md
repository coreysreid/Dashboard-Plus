# Core needs and source boundary

Checked **21 November 2026**: shipped `modules/medical/` and `modules/people/`, `docs/DESIGN.md` §6, decisions 0089 and 0100, and the shipped helper OpenAPI description. Medical currently has kits, kit checks and training; People's medication Important detail is the existing summary. This draft makes no claim about a medicine's dose, schedule or safety. The fictional Garcia example names no real drug.

**Why there are no loadable TOML files yet:** A declarative link to `people.important_info` does not currently constrain the target to `kind = medication` and the same recipient. It cannot safely select a private detail without a deliberate masked picker, and no approved immutable historical medicine-label contract exists. A file that loaded would accept wrong-person or wrong-kind links and could leak a private label through a picker or record name. Build those core capabilities and a migration/retention plan first, then write the Medical extension's tables, labels, migration and private-sheet policy. Do not write a workaround that copies the People text into an ordinary field.

`datetime` needs a durable instant, originating zone and household-local display rule for entries from a phone in another zone. Idempotent saves, Review revalidation, a possible-repeat warning, mistaken-entry audit and member acceptance state are core work. Decide whether private dose details should travel to every paired member under the medical exception: the proposal says yes, explicitly, for safety continuity, but the household must see that choice before pairing. Helpers stay excluded until a separate medical permission review. No real-device or clinical test has been run.
