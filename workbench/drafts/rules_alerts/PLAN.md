# A household's own rules, in plain words

Batch 24 item 135, planned **28 November 2026**. This is a proposed feature, not a rule engine that has shipped. Dashboard Plus already shows Stock below **Keep at least** and dates due soon on **Needs attention**. A new household rule must add a useful observation from records the household chose, not duplicate those reminders, diagnose a pet, or turn a day without an entry into a failure.

## The first screen

**Settings → My rules** starts with **No rules of your own yet. You can ask Dashboard to point out a change in records you chose. It can only compare what you recorded.** Choose **Make a rule**. The screen builds one sentence in this order:

> **When** [Rex's recorded weight] **is** [at least 10% lower] **than** [a recorded weight 25 to 35 days earlier], **show me** [a note on Needs attention].

The names, comparison and dates are selected from checked choices; they are not a free-form formula. **Rex** and **10%** are invented household choices for this worked example, not a veterinary threshold, diagnosis or recommended setting. Nothing is prefilled with 10%. Before Save, the preview shows what evidence exists: **2 of Rex's weigh-ins can be compared for this rule. One is on 1 October and one is on 31 October. No weight between them is guessed.** The person can choose **Save rule**, **Change choices** or **Cancel**. A saved rule can be paused, edited, deleted or undone without changing source records.

If those two fictional weights were 20.0 kg and 17.9 kg, the arithmetic is `(20.0 − 17.9) / 20.0 = 10.5%` lower. **Needs attention** would say:

> **A change you asked to notice · Rex.** Recorded weight: 20.0 kg on 1 October and 17.9 kg on 31 October (10.5% lower). **This compares two entries you made. It does not say why the weight changed.** [See both weigh-ins] [Pause this rule]

The card stays under **Your rules**, apart from existing due and low-stock sections, so it cannot be mistaken for an official health warning. It is not shown as **danger**, **unhealthy**, **missed**, **failed**, **you should have** or **safe**. A person who is concerned can use their own professional contact; the app gives no feeding or treatment instruction. If an old reading is corrected or undone, the card recalculates or goes away with a history note; it never persists a now-false claim. A repeated page load or restart does not make another card for the same pair of readings.

## Which records a rule may watch

| Source and first scope | What is honest | Gate and refusal |
|---|---|---|
| **Pets → Weigh-ins**, one exact pet, `day` and `weight` in kg | Compare two accepted, undeleted readings actually 25–35 household-calendar days apart. Show both dates, values, percent basis and source links. Same-day repeats are separate readings but do not create a month of history. | No interpolation, extrapolation, “normal weight” or medical conclusion. If there is no actual eligible earlier reading, say **Not enough dated weigh-ins for this rule yet**; do not fire. A person must choose the percent and window. |
| **Household stock → Keep at least**, one exact item | Reuse the existing minimum and its current quantity on Needs attention. | Guide the household to its existing **Keep at least** box, not a second rule and second card. Historical stock levels are not reconstructed from today's amount. |
| **Use-by and due dates** in Stock, Medical, Pets, Routines, Garden and Home care | Reuse their existing module `watch` rules and household date wording. | A custom rule cannot turn an empty date into overdue or say a future job was done. Do not duplicate an existing reminder under Your rules. |
| **Pet feeding**, later | Food **put out**, by one pet and one recorded unit, may be compared across household-confirmed complete periods. | Food put out is not food eaten; no missing-day or appetite alert until a complete-period fact exists. Pouches and grams never add together. |
| **Doses, health readings, money**, later | A checked source declaration could someday allow a household-defined observation. | An absent dose is not missed, a planned dose is not given, a partial price history is not spending, and no arbitrary health inference is allowed. Keep these out of the first builder. |

The first version should offer only the pet-weight comparison and links to existing Stock/date reminders. For a newly recorded weight, choose the eligible earlier reading closest to 30 days before it; if two days are equally close, choose the earlier day and show that choice. Never scan farther back for a pair that makes the rule fire. If either chosen day has multiple readings, the rule waits with **More than one weight was recorded on this day. Choose which reading to compare** rather than silently picking one. Widening the menu requires a reviewed declaration for each measure: exact table and subject link, actual dated numeric field, unit, time-window choices, source correction behavior and privacy classification. The rule engine must not accept a table name, SQL expression or unit supplied through a browser or helper request. A module switched off, subject deleted, missing link or source permission made local disables its derived rule with a neutral explanation. A restore rechecks rule identity and evidence before showing anything; it does not match a different Rex by name.

## A card's life and quiet hours

Each card represents one **condition episode** for one rule and one exact subject. The rule records which source revisions and two dates supported it. A newer pair that still meets the condition updates the card's evidence, not its count. A corrected or deleted reading retracts the card; a later eligible pair starts a new episode only after the condition has first ceased to hold. **Dismiss this note** hides this episode from the main list and leaves the rule on; **Pause rule** stops new evaluations until the person resumes it. Neither action edits a weigh-in. **Why am I seeing this?** opens the chosen sentence, arithmetic, dates and last evaluation time. This is a person-chosen observation, so it must never outrank an existing safety or due reminder by invented severity.

In the first release there are **no push, email or OS alerts**; Needs attention updates when the app evaluates its accepted local records, and the builder has **no Quiet hours control** because there is nothing to schedule. If a notification channel is built later, offer an off-by-default **Notify me** choice and household-chosen quiet start and end in the household's time zone, with **No notifications** always available. Quiet hours delay a notification; they do not hide a card when someone opens Needs attention or change its date. Daylight-saving changes use the household's zone, not the device clock's guessed zone. No rule is labelled too urgent to bypass the person's quiet choice. A second computer or phone may show only a filtered copy after the household has consented to sharing the underlying records and rule; a stale member says when its copy was last accepted.

## Privacy and wording boundaries

A pet's name may be ordinary in the current module, but a future rule about a private source may reveal more than that source's closed list. Store the rule configuration on this computer first. If a household later shares rules, decision 0140 needs its own explicit choice and a filtered calculation: a member must not infer a list kept here from a rule title, card existence, count, unit, threshold, source link, address, print, export, helper result or notification. A private rule is a neutral **Private rule · Show details** card until a session-checked POST Show; default print stays closed. Existing source values still follow their own Show boundary. Helpers cannot create, edit, dismiss or receive rule details in the first version.

Do not offer free-form rule names in the first version. If later offered, the same decision 0141 guard must refuse passwords, codes, full card/account numbers and ID details without echo on every form, import or helper path. Percent, period and subject ID are checked choices; neither a rule nor its evidence cache stores forbidden information. The local record and backup still deserve ordinary household protection; Show is a screen control, not encryption.

## Build order and checks

1. **Prove a one-source rule.** Define a checked rule declaration for `pets.weigh_ins` and a core-owned saved rule with stable IDs, source global IDs, chosen threshold/window, owner, paused state and history/Undo. Validate the declaration at load and revalidate each saved choice on use. On existing Stock/date choices, link to the shipped reminder rather than adding a rule.
2. **Show the working.** Calculate from accepted, undeleted rows only, with exact kg and household-calendar dates. Handle two eligible readings, three with a nearer reading, equal weight, zero/invalid basis, same-day readings, no earlier reading, correction, deletion, Undo, module off and restore. A baseline of zero is not divided by; the rule stays waiting with an explanation. A newer out-of-window reading cannot be silently paired with a convenient old one. Show the exact pair chosen so a household can disagree.
3. **Keep one episode.** Reopen Needs attention, restart and rerun evaluation: one card. Correct the supporting value, then restore it: retract and re-evaluate without phantom duplicate history. Pause, resume and dismiss each have distinct words and Undo. Existing Stock and due cards stay unchanged.
4. **Check privacy and sync.** Try a private future source and a locally kept source with invented sentinels. No closed page, address, helper API, sheet, print, export, sync snapshot or change log learns the rule or its derived fact unless the household's choice permits it. A rule never expands a source's sharing choice. A phone/member with stale accepted data says so and never evaluates missing rows as zero.
5. **Check the experience.** At 320 px and desktop, light/dark/high contrast and A4/Letter print, keyboard and screen reader, compare the builder sentence, card, arithmetic and source record links. The card says **recorded**, names both dates and units, and says nothing caused a change. An unrecorded day, insufficient history and a disabled rule are quiet, not blame.

The Rex dates, weights, percentages and 25–35-day window above are invented design examples, **not** a health threshold or veterinary advice. No rule engine, notification channel or quiet-hours setting was tested.
