# Collections draft

A household can group a hobby collection, give each item a name and optional set or catalogue number, record its condition and full Stock place, and enter what it cost, what they think it is worth now, and what it sold for. The current values are household estimates. They are not appraisals, sale prices or insurance replacement values. No external prices or ownership claims are included. The example rows use only made-up names and amounts.

**Total value needed from the core:** On a collection page, add `value_now` for nondeleted items with `status = owned`. Show the currency configured for the household and how many owned items have no estimate: **“Your estimated value now: $95 across 2 of 2 owned items.”** If one owned item has no value, say **“$60 across 1 of 2 owned items; one has no estimate.”** Do not treat a blank as zero or add `sold_for` to this total. Recompute after edits, sales, deletions and restores. Do not show a gain/loss claim from `bought_for` and `value_now` without asking what the household means by those figures. The current declarative core has no aggregate-card contract; the fields can be recorded, but the total needs core support.

The `status` choice and sale fields need a conditional form rule: show **Sold for** and **Sold on** only for `sold`, and refuse a sale amount on a still-owned item or a sold status without a sale date if Lane A wants consistent histories. The current declarative form can show all fields but cannot enforce this combination. A sale should keep the item's record and past value for history, while removing it from the current total. `value_now` should be allowed to stay as the last estimate but never counted after sale.

`place` links to shipped Stock Places today and should show its full path. If Home layout later owns shared Places, Lane A must migrate this link with the Stock place mapping and backup/way back; see Home layout `NOTES.md`. A household may keep collection values private, so printable sheets, export and helper access should be reviewed before promotion. This draft has no sheet. The People or Stock modules do not get a collection's value added to their totals.

The `money` field uses the core's household currency display. The examples do not assume an exchange rate or market value. Checked the shipped Stock place link and declarative `money` type against `docs/DESIGN.md` section 6 on **2026-11-17**. No outside factual source or third-party data is used.
