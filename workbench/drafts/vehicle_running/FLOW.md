**Vehicle page:** Under an existing Vehicles row, put **Odometer readings** with **Add a reading**. The latest accepted row says **Last recorded: 48,500 km on 20/11/26**; tap for earlier rows. It is the last *recorded*, not a verified current odometer. Show a newer pending phone row separately as **Saved on this phone · waiting for main**, never as accepted. A lower reading than the previous one shows **This is lower than the last reading. Check the vehicle and unit before saving.** Offer **Go back** and **Save as entered** for an actual rollover or correction; do not erase either reading. Mixed km/mi stays separate and cannot generate a mileage delta until a household makes a reviewed conversion decision.

**Existing service page:** Keep `vehicles.services.next_due` as the authoritative date typed from the household's schedule. Add **Cost estimates** with **Add estimate**. Rows identify **Dealer**, **Independent** or **Other**, provider, quoted date and optional **Quote good until**. **Show estimated cost and scope** deliberately reveals the private amount and scope. Compare side by side only after Show, with **Check what each quote includes. These are estimates, not a final price.** Never mark the cheapest as recommended or treat a quote as evidence that the work was done. A completed service still needs the existing service date to be changed by a person.
