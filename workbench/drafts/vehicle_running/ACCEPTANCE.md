# Vehicle running checks

Written **21 November 2026**. Use invented vehicles and quotes.

1. Load Smith, Garcia and Taylor rows. Each reading links to its vehicle and each estimate to an existing Vehicles service. An unknown service or vehicle is refused.
2. Two readings on the same date remain separate. A lower or mixed-unit reading prompts a check; no automatic mileage delta or service warning is computed. Correcting a mistaken reading preserves history.
3. Two estimates for one service display dealer and independent descriptions with their own quoted dates, scope and optional validity. No cheapest recommendation or claim of identical work appears.
4. A Vehicles service due in 12 days still creates its existing date reminder. The running draft can add only a dated last-reading and estimate count in detail; it cannot claim a distance-based due event without a target.
5. Plant invented amount/scope/notes sentinels. They stay behind Show and out of ordinary list, Needs attention, sheet, general export, helper read and portable change-log values. Backup keeps them locally.
6. On a member, amount and scope withheld from main are explicitly described as **Only on this computer**. Pending readings or quotes never replace accepted data before Review approval and pull.
7. Deleting a vehicle or service with linked history is refused until the household deliberately resolves the readings or quotes. No cascade quietly destroys a cost comparison or odometer record.
8. At 1,000 dated readings and 100 estimates, the vehicle page paginates, sorts by recorded time with a stable tie-breaker, and shows the latest accepted record without loading every row.
