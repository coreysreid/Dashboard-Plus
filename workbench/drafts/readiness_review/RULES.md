# Batch 21 item 100: when a record-backed question comes back

Checked 21 November 2026 against `modules/readiness/prompts.toml`, the shipped People, Home layout, Stock and Medical tables, `src/dashboard/core/questions.py`, and decision 0104. These are product rules for the eight `answered_by` questions. The other 41 questions keep their chosen review dates. A record is always an offer, never proof that a plan works.

## One rule for all eight

After a person answers **Yes** and explicitly chooses a record, ask again when that **chosen record** loses a field needed for the question, changes its identity or relevant location, stops matching, is deleted, or its module is switched off. Do not ask again just because some other matching record was added or edited. Do not ask on any record change after **Not yet**, **Not relevant**, or a **Yes** with no record chosen; those answers return on their chosen date. A change to Settings that makes a question not fit moves it to “Does not fit your settings” without changing the saved answer. When it fits again, apply the normal review rule.

Keep the chosen review date in force even when no record changes. If both date and record change apply, show the record-change reason once. Nothing repeats for each edit or each missed day. If a selected record is deleted or stops matching, do not silently swap to a different record. Offer the current matches again and let the person choose.

Use the existing generic reminder: **“The record you used for this answer changed. Does your answer still fit?”** For a deleted/missing record, use **“The record you used for this answer is no longer available. Does your answer still fit?”** Never put a private detail, number or access text in Home or Needs attention. On the question page, show **“This record may help. Does it answer the question?”** and allow **Yes**, **Not yet**, **Not relevant**, or Not now. A prior Yes stays in history; the looked-at count falls until the person answers again.

## Exact field decisions

“Ask again” below means when the field changes on the record the person chose, including a linked field or ancestor place as stated. A field cleared or changed so the record no longer matches always asks again. Editing an unrelated row does not.

| Question ID and words | Ask again for these changes | Do not ask again for these changes |
|---|---|---|
| `fire-alarm-location` — “Have you thought about where the smoke alarms are and when they were last checked?” | Chosen equipment's `kind`, `name`, `place`, `last_tested`; chosen place's name or parent, or any ancestor's name or parent; deletion. | `battery_changed`, `next_check`, equipment notes/access details, unrelated equipment, a new alarm. A scheduled check warning remains separate. |
| `flood-flood-utilities` — “Have you thought about where the household's utility shut-offs are if flood advice tells you to use them?” | Chosen shut-off's `kind`, `name`, `place`, `how_to_find`; chosen place path; deletion. `how_to_find` is ordinary directions, never an access code. | Notes/access details, a meter or exit edit, another shut-off. A change only to access details stays private and does not create a public prompt. |
| `heat-heat-water` — “Have you thought about where your household's drinking water would come from during very hot weather?” | Chosen lot's `item` link or `place`; its quantity **crossing to zero**; linked item's `category`, `drinking`, `unit` when the unit meaning changes, or `name`; chosen place path; deletion. If a batch split removes the chosen lot, ask for a new choice. | Using or adding water while the chosen lot remains positive; a unit spelling change within the same litre class; `minimum`, notes and use-by date. Use-by has its own Stock warning and this question does not certify drinkability. Another lot being added or used does not invalidate the chosen one. |
| `medical-kit-location` — “Have you thought about where a first aid kit is kept?” | Chosen kit's `name`, `kept` (home, vehicle or elsewhere), `place`; chosen place path; deletion. | `last_checked`, `next_check`, kit check history and notes, another kit. The check-due warning remains separate. |
| `medical-prescriptions` — “Have you thought about where current prescription details are recorded?” | Chosen Important detail's `kind`, `detail` or `person` link; linked person's `name` or `lives_here`; deletion. | That person's phone, birthday or unrelated notes; another medication detail. The detail itself remains private behind Show. |
| `medical-care-contact` — “Have you thought about whom to call for ongoing care needs?” | Chosen Who to call row's `role`, `person` link or `priority`; linked person's `name` or `phone`; deletion. | The person's email, birthday, address or notes, a different contact. If the phone changes, a new choice is useful even if the contact is still a doctor or pharmacist. |
| `medical-special-food` — “Have you thought about any food or feeding needs that differ from the household's usual supplies?” | Chosen Important detail's `kind`, `detail` or `person` link; linked person's `name` or `lives_here`; deletion. | That person's phone, birthday or unrelated notes; another dietary detail. Do not echo the dietary detail in the reminder. |
| `leaving-contact-away` — “Have you thought about who everyone would contact after leaving home?” | Chosen Who to call row's `role`, `person` link or `priority`; linked person's `name` or `phone`; deletion. | The person's email, birthday, address or notes, a different contact. A number change deserves one review, not a public display of the number. |

For every place path in the table, changing only a place note does not ask again. Moving a cupboard by changing its parent does: the full path the person relied on changes. A missing or deleted ancestor makes the chosen match invalid. A linked person/stock item edit touches only the listed fields, even though today's record revision changes for every edit.

## What differs from the shipped rule

Today `questions.py` fingerprints the whole record revision, plus the revisions of linked records and places it reads. Thus changing a kit's `last_checked`, using one litre from a still-positive water batch, or adding a harmless note brings the Yes question back. These field rules keep the reminder tied to the actual question. They do not reduce the annual or household-chosen date reminder.
