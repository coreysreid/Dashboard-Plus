# See the month on a calendar

**Things to do → Calendar** shows every dated list together for one month: Routines, Home care, the Vegetable
garden, vehicles, bills and policies, papers to review, first aid kit checks, smoke alarm tests and things lent out.

- Each mark has a symbol as well as a colour: **✓ Done**, **– Skipped**, **○ Due, not recorded as done yet**,
  **◇ Planned date**. The legend under the month says what each means.
- Under the grid, **"November 2026 in words, day by day"** lists the same days in words. Each line starts with its
  list: "Home care · Clean the gutters: Job done".
- Busy month? **Show:** keeps one list at a time ("Show: Vehicles").
- A blank day only means nothing was recorded or planned. **Nothing is ever called missed.** A late task shows once,
  on its own date, as "due since".
- A pet's page has its own **Feeding calendar**, and a person's page a **Dose calendar**. The dose calendar shows only
  how many doses each day until you press **Show**.
- Wrong earlier day? On a list's own calendar, **Not a done day — remove from the calendar** takes it off, with
  **Undo**. The record itself is not changed.

To print the words with the month, open the list's own calendar (the link under a record's small calendar) and print
that page.
