# Private details, and the Show button

Some details are private: medical details, medicine doses, prices, money back, a car's registration, a bill's
amount and reference. On screen they wait behind a **Show** button.

- **Show** is a button you press, not a link. What it shows is on that page only; going back or reloading hides it
  again, and the address never holds it.
- A private list, such as **Medicine doses**, says only how many until you press Show: "2 doses recorded". This holds
  on its calendar, bars and timeline too.
- Private details are never on Home, printed sheets (except the **Emergency sheet with private medical details**,
  which you choose), the spreadsheet export, or a helper's screen.
- Keep private things in their private fields. A medicine typed into a Stock item's name, or a code typed into a
  note, is ordinary information that shows everywhere.
