# Charts and bars: see how things change

- **A pet's weight:** on the pet's page, the weigh-ins are dots by date. Choose **Last 3 months**, **Last year** or
  **All dates**. The words say the change: "up 0.3 kg since 2 Oct". The scale starts at zero, so a small change is
  not made to look big. These are the weights written down, not a health check.
- **Bars over time:** one bar per day, week or month. Use **By day**, **By week**, **By month** and **Earlier**.
  - A pet's **Food put out**, in one unit at a time (grams or pouches, never added together or converted). Food
    put out is not food eaten.
  - A routine's **Times done**, a Home care job's **Work done**, the garden's **Jobs done**, a vehicle's **Services
    done**.
  - A person's **Doses recorded**, every medicine together, until you press **Show**.
- Every bar has its value in words, and the same values are in a table under it ("…, as a table").
- A day, week or month with nothing written down says **"nothing recorded"**. The bar for this week or month is
  striped and says **"so far"**.
- **Spent per month** (Household stock): see [what you paid](PRICES.md).
