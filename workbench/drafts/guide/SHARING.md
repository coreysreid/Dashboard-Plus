# What your other computers get

**Settings → What your other computers get** lists every list and each of its details. For each you choose
**Shared with your other computers** or **Only on this computer**, and for a list with private details, **Shared,
but not the amount** or **Shared, but not the private details**.

- Nothing is sent yet: computers cannot be paired yet. These choices decide what is kept ready to go.
- Each list starts as it always was, marked **Usual**. Money back starts as Only on this computer. Codes and access
  details are never shared.
- **Keeping something here** happens at once.
- **Starting to share something private** asks first, **"Start sharing these private things?"**, and says what will
  go and that a copy another computer gets cannot be taken back. **Undo** stops sharing it again from then on.
- **Earlier sharing choices** lists every change, with Undo where it applies.
- Helpers (assistants with a key) have their own permissions in Settings, Helpers.
