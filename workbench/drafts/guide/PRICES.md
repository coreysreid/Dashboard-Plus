# What you paid, and money back

- When you save a **bought** Stock in (or later, on its page), you can type **What you paid** and say whether it is
  the **total** or the **price for each one**. Leave it blank if you do not know. A Stock in without a price is
  counted, never read as zero.
- On **Review**, a helper may show the price it read from a receipt beside the row, for checking. It is saved only
  if you type it in and press **Accept**.
- **Spent per month** (on Household stock) counts your Stock ins. **Show** adds up the prices, overall and for each
  kind of thing, one currency at a time. It covers only the prices you typed in, so it is not everything the
  household spent.
- Got money back? On a Stock in with a price, **Record money back**. The month then says "3.80 AUD spent, less 3.00
  refunded". A refund never changes the stock: if the goods went back, also record a Stock out, **Returned to the
  shop**.
- Prices and money back are never on lists, printed sheets, the export or a helper's screen.
