# What happened: one list for a pet or a person

At the end of a pet's or person's page, **What happened, newest first** lists everything recorded for them under
month headings:

- weigh-ins with the change since the one before ("up 0.3 kg since 2 Oct");
- vet visits and treatments given;
- feedings as one line a day ("3 feedings recorded on Tue 24 Nov · 240 g put out");
- doses as one line a day that says only how many, until you press **Show**.

A vehicle's page lists its odometer readings and service quotes, a Home care job's page its work done, and a
routine's page each time it was done or skipped. **Earlier** goes back a page. Only what was written down and
accepted is listed: a gap means nothing was recorded, not that anything was missed.
