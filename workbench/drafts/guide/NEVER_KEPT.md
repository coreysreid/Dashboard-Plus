# What Dashboard Plus never keeps

Dashboard Plus helps you find what matters. It is not a safe for things that would let someone empty an account or
pretend to be you, so it never keeps:

- **passwords, PINs, alarm, door or key-safe codes**: write who to ask or where they are kept ("in the password
  manager", "ask Bob next door");
- **full card numbers, or full bank, account or policy numbers**: keep only the last 4 digits (••••1234);
- **copies or numbers of ID**: passports, licences, Medicare, tax file or social security numbers. Important
  documents records where a paper and its copies are kept, never the paper or its number.

If you type a full card number, it is refused with what to write instead. Anything like that saved before is hidden
until you tidy it on **Tidy sensitive details**, which makes a backup first and has Undo.

Remember: the data folder is not encrypted. Keep the computer or USB stick somewhere safe.
