# Bringing in an old tracker

Kept weigh-ins, feedings, doses or odometer readings in a spreadsheet or your phone's notes? On that list's page,
press **Bring in earlier entries**.

1. Save the spreadsheet as a CSV file, with the column headings in the first row, and choose it. The file is read
   on this computer only and is never saved.
2. **Match each column to a field.** Guesses are made from the headings; change any of them.
3. **Check the rows.** Every row is listed before anything is added:
   - **Rows that need fixing** say why ("Weight is in lb, and this list records kg. Nothing is converted").
   - **Names to match:** a pet or medicine name that matches nothing ("Rexy") becomes a choice. Nothing new is
     ever made.
   - **Possible repeats** (the same pet at the same time as one already recorded) are skipped unless you tick
     **Add anyway**.
   - **Dates that could be read either way** ("3/11/26") are listed; tick that they are read the way you meant.
4. Press **Bring in**. They are added at once, each with its own history, and the next page has **Undo** for the
   whole lot.

For doses you press **Show** first. Tips: put the amount and its unit in separate columns, and give every row a
date. This is different from **Import from a spreadsheet** in Settings, where rows wait on Review.
