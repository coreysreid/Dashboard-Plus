## Promotion order and way back

1. **Back up and record the starting state.** Capture Stock place IDs, parents, names, notes, Stock item links, Medical kit links, and their full paths, plus the existing Stock and Medical sheets. A backup is needed before changing a household's records. Work in one transaction or stop on any failed constraint.
2. **Create the target places.** Insert one Home layout place per old Stock place. Translate `inside` to `parent`; preserve the parent graph rather than flattening children. For the three examples, use the curated kinds in the CSV. For an unknown real place, do not guess “storage” or “spot” from its name; use a neutral kind or ask the household after migration. No suggestion from `places.csv` should pre-create or rename a household place.
3. **Repoint all references atomically.** `stock.items.place` and `medical.kits.place` use the old-to-new ID map. When lots ship, also repoint `stock.lots.place`. Search every installed draft promoted later (Collections, Vehicles, Home inventory and others) for links to `stock.places`; those need the same mapping before the old table can retire. Preserve item, kit and movement IDs and amounts.
4. **Add the new-only Home details.** The target `example.toml` adds the five new-only places after the imported rows. Exits, shut-offs, safety equipment and meters link to those or to imported places. This is demo content, not a migration step for a real household: do not invent an exit, utility meter or safety item while migrating someone's records.
5. **Compare before committing.** Every old place has exactly one target. The full path, direct linked-item count and kit link in the CSV match. Stock and Medical lists, forms, Needs attention and sheets display the new full path; quantities, dates and `kept` answers stay unchanged. The Our home sheet lists the household's own exits, shut-offs, safety equipment and meters with full paths and a printed date. Do not call it an emergency reference until its routes and locations are checked by the household.
6. **Finish or roll back.** Only retire Stock's place table after no live link points to it. Keep a reversible old-ID map and pre-migration backup. A failure or interrupted upgrade leaves the original database usable or restores it from that backup; retrying never duplicates places. Test restoration into a separate folder before relying on a backup.

The target Home layout `places` table declares `parent = "parent"`. Without that declaration, the generic formatter does not know to show a full path. The old-to-new map covers examples exactly; a real household may have duplicate short names or different nesting, so **IDs and parent links**, not name matching, drive migration. This is product and migration design from the local records; no external safety guidance is asserted.
