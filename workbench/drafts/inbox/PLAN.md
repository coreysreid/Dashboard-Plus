# An Inbox that keeps the decision, not the picture

Batch 24 item 136. Planned **28 November 2026**. The Inbox and photo intake are **not shipped**. Important documents currently records where a paper is kept, its kind and an optional review date; it never stores the paper, scan or number. A helper can already put some receipt-derived Stock suggestions on Review, but Dashboard does not read a receipt photo. This plan keeps those boundaries and decision 0141's rule against storing ID copies, full card or bank numbers, passwords and codes.

## A household's path

1. Choose **Bring in a receipt or letter**. The first screen says: **“Keep the original where you normally do. Dashboard will suggest only a few details for you to check. It will not keep the picture or the whole letter.”** Choose **Receipt** or **Letter**. A third choice, **Identity, payment or access paper**, says **“Do not add a picture of this here. Record where it is kept instead.”** and links to Important documents. A title or file name alone never decides that a picture is safe.
2. First offer **Paste text after removing private numbers**. A photo option comes only after a local, temporary image pipeline passes the checks below. **Use camera now** should avoid deliberately saving to the camera roll; **Choose an existing photo** warns that the original still exists in that photo library. A phone, browser or operating system may keep its own copy; Dashboard must not promise to erase another app's copy. Nothing is sent to an online recogniser by the built-in flow.
3. Before any persistent Inbox or Review row, decode one bounded picture **in memory only**, discard metadata, read it locally, separate candidate fields and screen the *whole* extracted text and image class for forbidden detail. No raw image, thumbnail, crop, OCR transcript, EXIF, filename, preview frame or error excerpt may be written to the data folder, temporary files, logs, browser history, audit, backup, sync log, export or helper output. A failed check says **“This picture may include details Dashboard never keeps. Nothing was saved. Remove those details from a copy outside Dashboard, or add a short record by hand.”** It never repeats a suspected number or secret. If image-only or unfamiliar text cannot be screened reliably, the photo route stays off; paste or manual entry remains available. Detection is a gate to test, not a promise that OCR can identify every ID or secret.
4. A safe **receipt** preview shows only proposed goods, units, purchase day and an optional source line clipped to the product words. It leaves payment lines, loyalty numbers, totals, tax and discounts out of Stock movement text. Existing Stock items come first; a missing or ambiguous good asks **Choose item** or **Skip line**. A date, place, use-by day, drinking-water flag or price is never guessed. If a price is legible, the person's separate **Save a price with this Stock in?** choice is required; source price is never a confirmed purchase amount. A possible duplicate receipt gets an explicit **Check an earlier receipt** link before another Stock in is accepted.
5. A safe **letter** preview offers at most one proposed Important documents row: a short household-chosen name, kind and a date **only when the person confirms what that date means**. It asks **Where is the paper kept?** and may leave the date blank. It never stores the letter's body, account/policy number, signature, identity image or contact details lifted from the paper. A bill-like letter may later propose a Bills reminder, but **Due on** does not mean **Paid** or cover active; that target needs its own reviewed flow. If the type is uncertain, say **“We could not make a safe suggestion. The picture was not kept. Add a record by hand if you want one.”**
6. Press **Send these safe suggestions to Review**. The Inbox shows **3 rows waiting; nothing added to Stock or Important documents yet**, with the source type and day, not a private excerpt. Review names each proposed target, its exact fields and **Picture not kept; keep the original beside you while checking**. A person may Accept, Change or Reject each row; an unresolved line cannot become a Stock movement. Group acceptance and Undo must remain atomic where a new Stock item and its first movement depend on one another. Reject or finish discards safe source fragments; only the accepted target record and a minimal action/outcome audit remain. Reopening Review cannot recreate the discarded image.
7. **Inbox empty** says **“No safe suggestions are waiting. Pictures you tried to add were not kept.”** A closed or switched-off target module offers **Turn on this part or add it by hand later**; no queued proposal is silently retargeted to a similarly named record. The household can discard waiting safe rows at any time. A full queue refuses new intake without losing an existing row.

The first release can ship the paste/manual path while the photo gate is under test. Its screen must label photo intake **not available yet**; it must not use a hidden online OCR service as a shortcut.

## What a helper may do

A helper that the household has separately granted Stock access may read a receipt photo **in the helper's own app**, then send only allowed structured suggestions for Review. Dashboard never receives that image through the connector. Before sharing a photo with an online helper, say **“This helper may send the picture to its own service. Cover payment, loyalty, address and identity details first. Dashboard cannot remove a copy held by that service.”** A helper without the relevant grant cannot list records or stage them. A helper may not upload an Inbox image, read local-only waiting rows, choose the person's missing date/place/price, accept its own proposal, or bypass the whole-request forbidden-detail guard. Untrusted words in an OCR line or letter are source data, never instructions to the helper or a rule for Dashboard. The built-in local route and an external helper's route need separate labels; **“stays on this computer”** applies only to the former.

If Documents becomes a helper target, it needs a specific grant and a safe field contract: name, kind, optional chosen holder/place/review date; no file, full number or document body. Until that contract exists, a helper may discuss a letter in its own app but cannot stage a Documents row. Sharing choices between household computers (0140) are separate from helper permissions. The pending Inbox list starts **Only on this computer**; an accepted Stock or Documents record follows that target list's effective sharing choice, with the first-pairing consent preview. Neither choice ever authorises sharing a raw picture that the app does not keep.

## Storage and time limits to prototype

The candidate intake is **one JPEG or PNG, at most 10 MB and 20 million pixels**, or bounded pasted text. Unsupported HEIC, PDF, multi-page, encrypted, malformed or oversized input stops before OCR with **“This file is not supported here. Nothing was saved.”** This is a proposed first limit, not evidence those formats are safe or that a particular phone converts them locally. The parser needs byte, pixel, decoded-text, line, processing-time and queue limits before it is exposed to household files; a decompression bomb or hanging OCR must not fill the computer. No external request, image cache, exception dump or debug capture is allowed.

Only **safe, structured proposed fields** may be persisted: target table, target action, selected record ID, proposed values, a bounded non-payment product fragment when useful for checking, local receipt-group ID, creation time and Review state. Candidate limits: at most 50 safe lines per intake and 100 pending groups. At 30 days, show **“This suggestion has waited 30 days. Check it or discard it.”**; do not silently delete unfinished work. A completed or rejected group loses source fragments immediately, while its minimal status, target IDs and decision time follow the normal Review history policy. Never retain an image hash as a substitute for deleting the image. Duplicate detection should use a bounded canonical basket of approved Stock facts, not raw photo bytes or unfiltered OCR text.

## Build order and acceptance

1. Add a **text-only local** prototype with an explicit Receipt/Letter choice and one allowlisted target at a time. Validate a pasted invented product line and a short invented renewal notice before staging; inspect the database, backups, logs, export and helper output for only the proposed safe fields. A refusal leaves zero Inbox or Review rows. Confirm the target module is on and permissions are current at **both** staging and Accept.
2. Build the temporary photo boundary separately. Test a plain invented receipt, a letter with a full account number in its heading, a visible card number, an ID photo, a QR/barcode with embedded text, metadata containing a name or location, a handwritten secret, Unicode-spaced digits and a hidden second page. A detected or uncertain forbidden value is refused **before** any persistent row. Search process temp, browser cache, database, backups, sync log, reports and crash diagnostics for unique sentinels after success, rejection, error and forced quit. If the environment spools input to disk, redesign before enabling photos.
3. For a receipt, use the invented two-line example in `workbench/drafts/receipt_entry/`: one clear Stock in, one uncertain line. Check **Choose item**, **Skip line**, pack size versus quantity, dates and units. An offered source price remains Review-only until the person chooses to save it. Repeating the same request and submitting a similar basket do not silently duplicate or discard a real purchase. Reject and Undo restore exact Stock amounts.
4. For a letter, stage an invented warranty location and a separately chosen review date. A passport, licence, bank statement, card letter, code or full policy number never becomes a Documents attachment or free-text field. An ambiguous date stays blank; a Bills due date never claims payment. Reject leaves Documents unchanged, and a deleted or switched-off target asks for a fresh choice rather than a name guess.
5. Try an online helper and a local helper, each with Look-only, Look-and-suggest and no target permission. The household sees which service can receive the image **before** sending. The API's whole-request guard catches forbidden text in `source`, `why` and proposed fields without echo. A helper cannot accept Review or obtain a raw Inbox image. Review labels helper words as a suggestion, not as an instruction.
6. At 320 px and desktop, keyboard, screen reader, dark mode and print, check every state: nothing saved, waiting, accepted, rejected, unsafe/unsupported and queue full. Before Show, no private name, source line or amount escapes in list HTML, URL, print, search, export, connector, sync snapshot or browser Back. Print defaults to a count, not the discarded picture. The person can read and choose without the graphic.
