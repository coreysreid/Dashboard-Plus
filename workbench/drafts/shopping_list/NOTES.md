# Core work needed for a shopping trip

Checked 18 November 2026. The current core has Stock items, batches, movements, running-low watch and a single Stock-in form. A bought movement with no chosen batch starts a new batch with its own date and place; decision 0057 makes Which batch follow the selected item. It has no persistent shopping trip/line record or atomic multi-movement trip save. The household flow in FLOW.md needs:

1. A trip and trip-line model with the source Stock item ID, a snapshot of its quantity, unit and minimum at list creation, suggested and edited buy amounts, aisle, picked-up state, actual brought-home amount and final movement and Stock batch (`lot`) IDs. Keep a stable distinction between plan and purchase.
2. A calculation service using decimal arithmetic and the item's own unit. Do not convert packages to kg or litres without a confirmed pack size. An item whose minimum or unit changes after list creation must be flagged for review; do not overwrite a person's edited plan.
3. A household-editable aisle mapping that does not alter Stock category. Aisles vary by shop, so no universal aisle claim is made here. A print-friendly view and keyboard-labelled checkboxes use the same trip state.
4. An atomic **trip commit** through the shipped Stock movement adjustment service. Save one bought movement per accepted batch row, with that batch’s own use-by date and full-path place. Link each trip line to its movement and lot ID, show new totals, and support a reviewed group undo with conflict detection. The trip commit ID is not a Stock batch ID; core `batch_id` groups audit/undo work and must not be confused with `stock.lots.id`. Idempotency must survive refresh or restart. If any row fails, roll the whole trip back.
5. Review validation: the split amounts for an item sum to its actual brought-home amount, a chosen existing batch belongs to that item, and date/place fields appear only for a new batch. Do not infer a catalogue shelf life or make an item drinkable from a shopping tick.
6. A safe way to make a manual one-off row without changing an existing item's Keep at least. A new Stock item should use the normal picker and validation before final Stock in.

The list should not be used as an emergency-supply recommendation. It reflects only the household's chosen thresholds. The [Stock labels](../../../modules/stock/labels.toml) and [J2](../../../docs/JOURNEYS.md) are the product sources, checked 18 November 2026.
