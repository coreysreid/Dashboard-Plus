Helpers

A helper is a program, such as an AI assistant, that can look at parts of your records and suggest new ones. Nothing a helper suggests is added until you accept it on the Review page. Private details are never shared with helpers.

Helpers connect only from this computer, with no internet needed for that. But if the helper uses an online AI service, whatever you let it read (the parts you tick below) is sent to that service. Tick only what it needs. A helper that runs entirely on this computer sends nothing anywhere.

Treat a helper's key like a password: don't paste it into chats or emails. If it might have been seen, turn that helper's access off and make a new one.
