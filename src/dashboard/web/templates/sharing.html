How these choices work

  • Nothing is sent yet. Pairing your computers is not built yet; these choices decide what is kept ready to go to them.
  • Keeping something on this computer happens at once, and anything already waiting to go is held back too.
  • Starting to share something private asks you first, says what will start going, and can be undone.
  • Choices count from now on. Anything another computer already has is not taken back from it. When your computers are paired, the first full copy they get follows the choices you have then.
  • Each list starts the way its part of the program set it up, marked "Usual" below. Codes and access details are never shared.

Helpers (assistants you gave a key) have their own permissions: Settings, Helpers.
