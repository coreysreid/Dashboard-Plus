Private details are left out

Anything you marked private, such as medical notes or access codes, is not in the export. The program's own keys and passwords are never in it either. Everything else is: names, phone numbers, addresses and where things are kept. Keep the file somewhere private, and do not email it.

This is not a backup

An export is a copy for reading and sorting in a spreadsheet. You cannot restore Dashboard Plus from it. Your backups are made for you automatically; see Settings, section Backups.
